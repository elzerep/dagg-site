## 3. Locked interaction hierarchy

1. The primary story never depends on exploratory clicking.
2. Desktop navigation may preview on hover and keyboard focus; activation
   navigates. Touch uses explicit disclosure.
3. Multi-step mechanisms use scroll-led discrete state progression or a finite
   causal one-pass sequence. WorkGraph and Factory are the two primary visual
   carriers.
4. Click/tap is reserved for navigation, CTA, form intent and optional deeper
   inspection.
5. Autonomous motion stops offscreen, pauses on hover/focus, and never loops
   endlessly.
6. Mobile/narrow tablet uses ordered scroll-triggered states or short finite
   sequences instead of heavy sticky carriers.
7. Reduced motion shows the resolved state immediately. No-JS exposes the full
   semantic story in reading order.
