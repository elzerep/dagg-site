## 4. Visual integration rules

- One route has one dominant visual job. Do not repeat the Home Hero grammar on
  every route.
- Decision Field surrounds and explains; Operational Evidence proves inside a
  bounded artifact; Machine Signal transitions a causal state and stops.
- No section uses more than two image-language roles, and Hero uses one.
- Reject generic AI lightshow, terminal walls, dashboard grids, node maps,
  circuit boards, Lego/architecture renders, Japanese-art pastiche, biological
  ambiguity, anonymous stock people and empty luxury minimalism.
- Images may be large and atmospheric where they carry the narrative; they may
  not become interpretation puzzles or decorative filler.
- Preserve the accepted palette, type, grid, Dagg logo and component language.

## 5. Interaction rules

- No causal story requires a click.
- Scroll-led carriers reverse deterministically where appropriate. Finite
  sequences stop and remain stable; they do not loop as ambient wallpaper.
- Desktop hover/focus navigation may preview. Activation navigates. Escape
  closes and restores focus.
- Touch uses explicit disclosure and does not inherit hover assumptions.
- Optional controls remain keyboard and touch operable.
- Reduced motion resolves immediately. Missing enhancement APIs do the same.
- No-JavaScript preserves every public sentence, route and causal state in
  semantic reading order.

## 7. Executable matrix

Use one immutable snapshot for every result.

### Viewports

- 320 x 720
- 360 x 800
- 390 x 844
- 768 x 1024
- 1024 x 768
- 1280 x 720
- 1366 x 768
- 1440 x 900

### Route and visual checks

- every public route loads the intended H1 and no route is a 404;
- headline-only read-through forms the route book in §2;
- Home preserves all six acts and the FS3 visual hierarchy;
- no horizontal overflow, clipped essential copy, broken crop, blank media,
  unreadable overlay text or hidden Hero;
- public type is at least 12 px;
- required desktop targets are at least 44 x 44 px and touch targets at least
  48 x 48 px where the shared system specifies it;
- 200 percent zoom/reflow does not remove the route, CTA or disclosure path;
- mobile and iPad are authored linear compositions, not squeezed desktop.

### Interaction and accessibility checks

- adaptive desktop navigation: pointer, keyboard, Escape and focus restore;
- mobile disclosure: open, focus containment, background inert, close and
  restore;
- all links and local anchors resolve;
- Transformation, WorkGraph and Factory sequences reach every state from
  zero-click scrolling;
- Pause, Replay and optional inspection controls are keyboard and touch usable;
- reduced motion, missing observer and no-JavaScript paths render the complete
  resolved meaning;
- headings, regions, form labels, status messages and disclosures have
  inspectable semantic names;
- contrast and visible focus pass for every tested background pair;
- no console error in any tested route or state.

### Conversion and truth checks

- every Start an assessment action reaches the Assessment route;
- validation, non-transmitting local-preview state and confirmation wording are
  truthful;
- no real inquiry is sent during QA;
- claims match `EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md`;
- Impact has no invented result and remains absent from public navigation;
- no named client, operating claim or outcome appears without permissioned
  evidence.

### Performance checks

- list transfer size by route and asset;
- Hero imagery has intrinsic dimensions and responsive sources;
- no raw production image is served when an optimized derivative is available;
- fonts, scripts and above-fold media do not introduce visible layout shift;
- record controlled LCP, CLS and interaction timing as evidence, not as an
  unsupported production claim.
