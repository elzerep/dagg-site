Status: passed 31 August 2026; released to P4R6  
Authority: Christian's 31 August sequencing correction, the two approved PDFs,
P4R3 pre-slice contract and P4R4 audit evidence  
Supersedes: P4R4 as the active implementation package; P4R4 remains diagnostic
evidence and is not one of the three final Home directions

## 1. Outcome

Build a real, responsive, interactive specimen of the Dagg Component and
Interaction Library before building the three Home vertical slices. This is not
a marketing homepage. It is the common implementation substrate that all three
directions must use.

The specimen must be good enough to answer, in the browser:

- what each component looks like;
- how it behaves at rest, hover, focus, active, open, changed and resolved;
- how it recomposes on mobile;
- what survives without JavaScript;
- how reduced motion preserves the same meaning;
- which content, image-language and truth-boundary rules constrain it.

radii, focus and motion tokens. The following never vary between the three
future Home directions:

- Paper, Lift, Panel, Ink, Coral, Coral Deep and Sage semantics;
- Geist, Newsreader and JetBrains Mono roles;
- 1240 px field, 12 columns and 940 px collapse;

## 4. Specimen inventory

### 4.1 Global chrome

Show and implement:

- warm desktop header;
- ink desktop header;
- compact desktop header;
- mobile closed and mobile open states;
- Dagg brand, Transformation, WorkGraph, Build, Impact, Company and persistent
  `Start an assessment` action;
- a Build flyout whose trigger is the word `Build` plus a proper icon-library
  chevron, never a text plus sign or handcrafted icon;
- hover intent 70-100 ms, close grace 180-240 ms, focus within, Enter, Space,
  Escape and focus restoration;
- adaptive flyout width and content hierarchy inspired by the best peer
  behavior without copying their visual identity;
- every destination remains a real link.

The flyout contains: Build overview / Dagg Factory / Agents and software / Trust
and operation. It is a stateful product preview, not a static link list beside a
single marketing card. Hover and keyboard focus on each destination update one
right-hand proof inset:

- Build overview: strategic decision → bounded build brief;
- Dagg Factory: requirement → product decision → evaluation;
- Agents and software: agent around an existing system ↔ rebuilt workflow
  layer;
- Trust and operation: proposed action → governed boundary → accountable human
  decision.

The first proof state carries the conclusion: `One operating decision becomes
a buildable, governed system.` The right-hand region may morph its dimensions
and composition in 180-240 ms, but the outer panel may not jump, flicker or
reposition. Crossfade content in 100-140 ms. Each state has one focal change,
uses actual Dagg components and remains a contained proof rather than a fake
miniature application.

Pointer movement from the trigger through the menu and into the preview must
stay inside a forgiving corridor. Closing uses the specified grace period.
Keyboard focus produces the same four states; Escape closes and restores focus.
Reduced motion swaps states without morphing. On touch/mobile, the four previews
become one-at-a-time disclosures inside the reading sheet. A permanently static
right-hand inset is a P0 failure.

### 4.2 Type, actions and links

Show H1, H2, H3, lead, body and metadata in real Dagg examples; primary,
secondary and text-link actions on warm and ink grounds; rest, hover,
focus-visible and active states. No pills. No ambiguous labels.

### 4.3 Three composition archetypes

The shared library supports exactly three opening compositions for the future
direction gate:

- `Strategic editorial`: asymmetrical 7/5 opening with a bounded Decision Field;
- `Product mechanism`: conclusion above or beside one inspectable carrier;
- `Operational consequence`: institution-level conclusion with one concrete
  governed state and visible provenance.

These are layout variants inside one system, not separate brands. Use concise
placeholder conclusions from the active Dagg copy only to prove scale and line
length. Do not present them as three finished pages in the specimen.

Render each opening fixture at both 1440 and 390 px. For these fixtures: H1 at
most 10 words, hero body at most 26 words, first viewport at most 55 visible
words, H2 at most 12 words and section introduction at most 32 words. Do not use
lorem ipsum, capability lists or explanatory copy that merely describes an
adjacent mechanism.

The specimen must also prove that these openings can flow into the following
shared section archetypes without new tokens or one-off CSS: short punctuation
(240-360 px), mechanism split (4/8), product stage (45/55), dominant proof with
a narrow rail (approximately 3/1), contained operational act, impact evidence
split and a 5/7 assessment close. No two image-heavy acts may follow one another
and a complete future Home direction may use at most three ground changes.

### 4.5 Strategic decision

Implement Preserve / Simplify / Automate / Rebuild / Retire as a directional
decision component, not a five-column table. Complete content is present without
JavaScript. One selected state changes a single explanation and proof marker.

### 4.6 WorkGraph record

Implement source selection for Meetings / Messages / Documents / Systems and
one retained record with Source / Work / Decision / Owner / Boundary / Evidence.
The record is contained Operational Evidence beside a smaller Decision Field
context carrier. Buttons are visible only in enhanced mode; without JavaScript,
all records remain readable and no inert controls remain.

### 4.7 Factory build progression

Implement Requirement / Product decision / Architecture / Build / Evaluation /
Review. The interaction is finite and user-controlled, or has pause/replay if an
automatic pass exceeds one second. It ends in an inspectable resolved state.
If an automatic evidence pass lasts six to eight seconds, pause and replay are
mandatory. Reduced motion resolves to the same verified end state immediately.

### 4.8 One operating model, two surfaces

Implement one carrier with two modes:

- `Inside the company`: governed agents carry bounded execution and return
  evidence to the company record;
- `At the customer surface`: a natural-language request reaches the same
  governed capability through Native product / Claude / ChatGPT / Codex.

This carrier is contained Operational Evidence, not a fourth visual language,
a full-screen dark dashboard or a vendor-style chat. It keeps one persistent
record, visible provenance and a truth boundary. Coral appears once at the
material judgment; sage appears only when the governed boundary becomes active;
there is at most one moving focal point.

The customer-surface state must show:

- Request: `What needs attention, and what can be resolved now?`
- Connection: `MCP exposes approved tools and permissioned context.`
- Authority: `The client system remains the source of truth.`
- Action: `Explain, prepare or execute only within the granted boundary.`
- Boundary: `No external write or commitment without accountable approval.`

This is not a generic chat interface and must not resemble or imply endorsement
by a vendor. WorkGraph, Factory and the customer harness remain the governed
source of context, rules, permissions, data and actions.

Changing mode must change context, action, permission and evidence in the same
carrier, not merely its heading. The shared center is a persistent company
record. Inside it moves through trigger / agent proposal / human checkpoint /
bounded action / recorded evidence. At the customer surface it moves through a
neutral request / the same governed context / source-grounded response /
permissioned action / the same updated record. The interface changes; context,
permissions and accountability do not.

## 5. Responsive and accessibility contract

Verify 1440, 1024, 768, 390, 360 and 320 px. Reading order is stable; mobile is
recomposed. There is no document-level horizontal overflow. H1 examples stay
within 2.5 lines. Body copy is at least 17 px. Hidden states are not focusable.
The full keyboard path works. Anchor targets clear the sticky header.

No-JS exposes all core content and routes without dead buttons. Reduced motion
shows the same resolved meaning immediately. Console errors are zero.

Coral/Paper and Sage/Paper are graphic combinations only, never body-text
pairs. Verify text and control contrast in rest, hover, focus and active states;
meaningful alt/figcaption treatment; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and
`aria-current`; mobile focus containment, Escape, focus return, body-scroll
unlock, touch activation and 200% zoom.

No-JS is tested from a fresh navigation with JavaScript disabled. Reduced
motion is emulated before page load. Every header, flyout, footer and action
route plus both Assessment CTA fixtures must resolve without 404, hash-only or
no-op behavior. The specimen makes zero remote requests, including fonts,
scripts and imagery.

Use native disclosure where possible. If controls switch panels, use tablist
semantics and roving tabindex; if they change the mode of one object, use a
pressed-button group. Hidden states use `hidden`, are not focusable and never
duplicate the reading order.

Performance budgets: initial JavaScript at most 120 KB gzip, navigation at most
20 KB gzip, CSS at most 45 KB gzip and initial mobile transfer at most 1.2 MB.
The hero asset is at most 500 KB on desktop and 300 KB on mobile, with explicit
dimensions and `sizes`.

Measure source budgets using deterministic gzip. Measure mobile transfer on a
fresh-cache 390 px load including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and loaded media;
confirm that `<picture>` loads only the appropriate hero source. The 70/25/5
image-language and page-color targets apply to composed full-page directions,
not to the component catalog.

## 6. Visual acceptance

Reject if the specimen permits or normalizes:

- `Build+` or text-symbol controls;
- terminal walls, circuit-board graphs or generic AI dashboards;
- repeated interchangeable black panels;
- five-cell tables posing as strategic interaction;
- empty whitespace without hierarchy;
- decorative motion or unbounded autoplay;
- cropped-away semantic events;
- image slots whose captions are required to explain them;
- components that would make all three future slices look the same.

## 8. Gate into the three Home directions

Claude self-reviews first. Codex independently reviews the same rendered states
against the two PDFs and fresh peer evidence. The specimen passes only when all
P0/P1/P2 issues are resolved and every interaction/state has named evidence.
It must also emit a machine-readable state and acceptance matrix plus exact
screenshots for closed, open, focus, mobile, reduced-motion and no-JavaScript
states. Evidence filenames must contain the actual measured viewport; do not
label a larger capture as 390 px.

Every acceptance record includes URL, timestamp, CSS viewport, DPR, screenshot
pixel dimensions, JavaScript state, motion preference and source snapshot hash.
Filename dimensions are derived from the measured values and any mismatch
fails the run. Reference comparisons must use a genuinely equal CSS viewport;
the existing peer files whose labels and pixel widths differ are research only,
not comparison-gate evidence.

After it passes, build three complete Home vertical slices:

- A - strategic/editorial;
- B - product/mechanism;
- C - operational/enterprise.

All three use this one component system but may vary commercial entry point,
final copy, section rhythm, primary proof and opening composition. Christian
chooses only after all three are complete, jointly reviewed and shown together.

`component-library/styles.css` and `component-library/script.js` are the
canonical shared layer for this gate. All six internal `X0` slices and all three
`X1` candidates import them; they may add composition-specific files but may not
copy the header DOM, duplicate tokens or fork component behavior. Identical
shared-file hashes are part of the A/B/C acceptance evidence.

Each direction uses a two-cut, cross-review protocol:

1. Codex and Claude receive the same frozen direction brief, approved PDFs,
   peer evidence, copy budget and acceptance matrix.
2. Codex produces an independent `X0-Codex` and Claude produces an independent
   `X0-Claude`; neither implementation may inspect the other during its first
   pass. They use separate owned files but the same library.
3. Claude reviews `X0-Codex`; Codex reviews `X0-Claude`. Both reviews must name
   visible evidence, failures and the strongest transferable decisions.
4. The program/design director creates one coherent `X1` from the winning
   decisions. This is not a compromise blend and must not combine incompatible
   rhythms or image roles.
5. Only the three verified `A1`, `B1` and `C1` directions are presented at the
   Christian selection gate. The six exploratory cuts remain internal evidence.

Do not deploy or publish.
