## 3. The three founder choices

### 01 — Operating Record

**First feeling:** governed execution and operating credibility.

**Static causal read:** heterogeneous company context crosses one Coral
judgment plane and one Sage permission boundary, then resolves into governed
work without losing provenance.

**Composition:** full-bleed Ink world, copy left, operating system right.

**Finite motion if selected:** one synchronized resolve through the shared
judgment and boundary, `0.8-1.0 s`, followed by stillness. Do not animate three
independent conveyor lanes.

**Reduced motion:** show the fully resolved state immediately.

**Founder chooses:** mechanism, execution and operational confidence first.

### 02 — Decision Field

**First feeling:** strategic judgment resolving company complexity.

**Static causal read:** fragmented company context becomes one coherent,
governed record through a single accountable decision and a single boundary.

**Composition:** warm Paper reading field fades into a large engineered Ink
environment; neither side is a separate card or dashboard.

**Finite motion if selected:** the context fragments consolidate once into the
retained record, `0.9-1.1 s`, followed by stillness.

**Reduced motion:** show the consolidated record and visible Coral/Sage
semantics immediately.

**Founder chooses:** the strongest balance of human judgment, strategy and
engineered capability.

### 03 — Continuous Intelligence

**First feeling:** the AI-native company learns and compounds through real
execution.

**Static causal read:** governed work produces denser evidence that visibly
returns through one shared boundary into the same retained company record.

**Composition:** cinematic Ink world, operating image left, copy right. This is
not a mirrored version of Option 01; it reverses both narrative direction and
page hierarchy.

**Finite motion if selected:** one evidence-return event moves toward the same
record, `0.9-1.2 s`, then stops. No continuous traffic, ambient loop or particle
field.

**Reduced motion:** show all evidence slips physically inserted beneath the top
record sheet with the denser retained marks already present.

**Founder chooses:** outcome, learning and compounding first.

## 4. Shared image-language rules

- Hero uses `Decision Field` as the primary language and only restrained
  `Machine Signal` as finite behavior.
- `Operational Evidence` does not become a third hero language. It enters later
  in the Home execution proof.
- Coral means accountable human judgment.
- Sage means governance, permission or boundary.
- Ink means machine precision and the operating environment.
- No hero uses all three image languages.
- No terminal, dashboard wallpaper, circuit board, server hardware, particle
  tunnel, generic AI spectacle, architecture model, material swatch display or
  biological association is permitted.
- Understand in three seconds, appreciate in ten, never need to decode.
