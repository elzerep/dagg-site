## 1. Outcome

Build one corrected Home slice at production-review quality. It must feel like
one Dagg system: Decision Field is the warm strategic world, Operational
Evidence is contained product proof and Machine Signal is a restrained finite
state change. Do not build more style directions.

The slice is Home, not the Transformation page and not a WorkGraph product
page. It is the executive summary of Dagg's complete company story.

## 2. Root correction

The image portfolios are generative grammars, not a fixed asset library. Every
slot begins with a written proof statement and receives an asset or coded
mechanism created for that exact job.

P4R3 failed because it made A, B and C into separate page identities. P4R4
uses them hierarchically:

- A surrounds and explains;
- C proves a concrete event inside a bounded instrument;
- B changes resolution inside A or C, then stops.

One section never uses all three. There is at most one dominant media object in
a viewport and never two image-heavy sections in sequence.

## 3. What survives from P4R3

From A:

- warm page world;
- 7/5 editorial opening;
- legibility, typography and decision continuum;
- light, calm navigation.

From B:

- immediate product specificity;
- contained dark proof surfaces;
- state changes that feel fast and exact.

From C:

- the stakes of human-speed versus machine-speed work;
- Operational Evidence for later Factory, governance and operating states;
- decisive contrast without turning the site into a dark hardware brand.

The A hero asset, B global page world, C hero object and C coral field do not
survive.

## 6. Interaction contract

- Existing P3 global chrome remains the shared navigation implementation.
- Build is the only desktop flyout. Hover intent 70-100 ms; close grace
  180-240 ms; Enter, Space and Escape work.
- Decision outcomes use native `details` so complete content survives no-JS.
- WorkGraph sources and impact modes have proper buttons, selected state and
  one carrier that changes in place.
- Factory exposes every stage without JavaScript, then enhances to a single
  selected state.
- No autoplay video. No ambient loop. One moving focal point in a viewport.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the same resolved meaning immediately.
- Every action is a real link or real state change. No fake route.

## 7. Responsive contract

- Verify 1440, 1024, 768, 390, 360 and 320 px.
- Reading order never changes.
- Mobile is recomposed: copy, proof statement, art-directed image crop and
  mechanism follow the same causal order.
- Hero remains at most 2.5 headline lines and body remains at least 17 px.
- No document-level horizontal overflow.
- Hidden states are not focusable.

## 8. Copy and density gates

- First viewport: at most 50 visible words outside navigation/actions.
- H1: 5-9 words. H2: 3-8 words.
- One sentence per default-visible section lead.
- Visible Home copy: 350-500 words.
- No capability list in the hero.
- No paragraph explains what an adjacent instrument already proves.
- No terminal wall, card inventory or generated image per section.
