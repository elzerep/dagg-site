# P1R2 · Vertical-slice copy polish

Status: accepted narrative amendment  
Authority: `../DAGG-GOLDEN-STANDARD-MASTERPLAN.md`  
Supersedes: P1R1 visible copy only where explicitly listed below

## 1. Purpose

Tighten the accepted vertical-slice copy after a publication-level narrative
and evidence review. This is a bounded language correction, not a reopening of
the company positioning, information architecture, navigation, section order,
WorkGraph prominence, CTA logic or selected visual direction.

## 2. Accepted amendments

### Evidence discipline

- Impact describes the evidence standard without implying that public cases
  are already approved or published.
- Factory compounding is described as system design intent, not a guaranteed
  outcome.

### Operating precision

- The operating thesis applies to execution, not to preserve, retire or
  no-build decisions.
- Action stops at a material exception; the exception itself is not described
  as stopping.
- Source-record disagreement is written as a condition, not as an active
  agent.

### Peer-level English

- `human release` becomes `human approval for release`.
- Decision criteria name value and success criteria precisely.
- The final decision sentence removes a dangling referent.

## 3. Explicitly unchanged

- Hero H1 and strategic register.

- Factory as the lead Build mechanism.

- All evidence limits and the ban on unsupported client or deployment claims.
