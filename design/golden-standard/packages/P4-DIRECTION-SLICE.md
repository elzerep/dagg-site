## 6. The three image and design directions

These are different visual systems, not colorways.

### Direction decision · 30 August 2026

Christian selected the following production hierarchy:

- **Primary:** A · The Decision Field for the Dagg brand world, hero,
  Transformation, strategic judgment and editorial concept imagery.
- **Contained proof mode:** C · Operational Evidence for real coded WorkGraph,
  Factory, governance, Trust and operating-record surfaces.
- **Restrained transition mode:** B · Machine Signal for meaning-bearing
  resolution and velocity transitions only.

Implementation must prove that hierarchy as one coherent vertical slice. It
must not alternate freely among three aesthetics. A surrounds and explains; C
proves; B may connect states. If a section cannot name which of those jobs it
performs, reject the visual treatment.

### A · The Decision Field

**Thesis**  
Dagg is the place where a warm human decision changes an exact machine field.

**Strategic fit**  
This direction best expresses human judgment, editorial intelligence and the
continuity from strategic map to governed operation. It carries the most
Anthropic-like warmth without copying Anthropic's collage motifs.

**Still-image grammar**

- high-resolution tactile material;
- warm fiber paper, graphite channels, vellum, pigment and matte clay;
- one coral intervention with semantic consequence;
- sage appears as a boundary or stabilizing layer;
- macro texture and generous quiet field;
- no literal device, screen or diagram inside the abstract image.

**Motion grammar**

- graphite structures become legible before a coral decision intervenes;
- a decision changes one path, boundary or material state;
- motion is slow enough to read and fast enough to imply intent;
- reduced motion shows the fully resolved field and the changed path.

**Composition**

- light editorial ground dominates;
- asymmetrical hero with one large tactile object;
- product artifacts sit nearby as exact ink instruments, not inside the
  generated image;
- editorial captions interpret the metaphor and lead into the concrete state.

**Primary risk**  
Too soft, precious or Anthropic-adjacent. Reject any scrapbook, lifestyle or
gallery feeling. The WorkGraph and Factory evidence must land immediately.

### B · Machine Signal

**Thesis**  
Dagg turns an organization from low-resolution human handoffs into a legible,
machine-speed operating signal.

**Strategic fit**  
This direction best expresses velocity, AI-native transformation and systems
that become more capable as context accumulates. It is the sharpest expression
of the widening gap between human-speed and machine-speed companies.

**Still-image grammar**

- deliberate resolution contrast: coarse raster becoming exact data;
- dither, scan, spectral edge, voxel or sampled field used as material, never
  as gaming or cyberpunk decoration;
- dark graphite and spectral white can dominate;
- coral is an intentional decision pulse, not a glow effect;
- sage is a stable verified band or bounded region;
- images may carry controlled low-resolution passages next to optically sharp
  areas.

**Motion grammar**

- sampled states resolve into a stable operating signal;
- speed is shown as reduced reconstruction and cleaner state transition, not
  fast particles;
- resolution and cadence change only when the record gains context or passes a
  decision;
- reduced motion shows coarse source and resolved result together.

**Composition**

- wide digital fields, cropped signal surfaces and stronger horizontal
  momentum;
- denser hero than A, but with one clear focal state;
- product artifacts may share the same raster cadence without becoming a
  dashboard;
- cooler derived neutrals are allowed while Dagg semantic colors retain their
  meanings.

**Primary risk**  
Generic AI, cyberpunk, circuit-board or crypto aesthetics. Reject electric
blue, ornamental particles, terminal cosplay and unreadable data fog.

### C · Operational Evidence

**Thesis**  
Dagg's confidence comes from a visible operating state: context resolved,
boundary triggered, human decision recorded, way back intact.

**Strategic fit**  
This direction best expresses Palantir-like implementation credibility and
xAI-like product confidence. It turns execution capability into the visual
center of gravity while keeping the strategic thesis spare.

**Still-image grammar**

- cinematic, dark and optically precise;
- night ink, smoked graphite, black ceramic, smoked glass and paper-white
  signal;
- restrained coral marks a material human decision;
- sage marks a verified or governed state;
- macro depth of field and controlled reflections;
- abstract evidence carriers are allowed; fake readable UI and fictional
  dashboards are not.

**Motion grammar**

- focus moves from evidence to boundary to accountable decision;
- states replace one another with measured optical transitions;
- one real coded product record supplies text and interaction;
- reduced motion shows the terminal verified state with the full record
  available beside it.

**Composition**

- dark instrument field carries the hero or proof surface;
- copy remains on warm paper or quiet night, never over noisy imagery;
- the payment-exception record is the primary visual object;
- abstract generated material supports the product artifact rather than
  impersonating it.

**Primary risk**  
Hardware launch, audio device, sci-fi gadget or hard enterprise software.
Reject gratuitous chrome, aggressive blackness and any generated interface
that could be mistaken for Dagg's product.

## 7. Accepted visual-system contract

The image portfolio is complete and the three families are now production
roles, not alternatives:

1. **Decision Field, approximately 70%.** The page world, hero, strategic
   judgment and editorial interpretation. Warm paper dominates.
2. **Operational Evidence, approximately 25%.** Contained WorkGraph, Factory,
   permission, governance and operating-record proof. It is an instrument
   inside the page, never a second website theme.
3. **Machine Signal, approximately 5%.** A short transition that makes
   resolution, velocity or inherited context legible. It is not a background,
   hero or section ground.

One section never uses all three. A visual treatment is rejected unless it can
name exactly one primary job: `world`, `instrument` or `transition`.

The generated source families remain conceptual images. They may not be
captioned as product screenshots, client data, deployments or outcome proof.
All product evidence is coded HTML using the accepted synthetic payment record
and its explicit provenance.

The selected hero master is
`direction-a-decision-field/01-hero-decision-field.png`. The older
`image-system/decision-field-hero-v2.png` and all files under `explorations/`
are non-production studies. Direction A's WorkGraph collage is not used in the
slice; WorkGraph is proved by the coded record. Direction C assets may support
art direction but never substitute for coded product proof. Direction B assets
are motion references only and never ship as a page section.

Every source family retains its `IMAGE-LANGUAGE.md`, dimensions and source PNGs.
The implementation derives responsive AVIF/WebP/JPEG delivery files without
overwriting the masters. Protected coral decisions, sage boundaries and the
causal relationship survive every crop.

## 9. Composition and editorial economy

- Use the accepted 12-column, 1240 px field and 7/5 or 45/55 splits from P2.
- At desktop, the hero occupies one viewport or slightly less below the header;
  the H1, lead, two actions, provenance and initial artifact state must all be
  legible without crowding.
- Warm grounds carry reading. Ink appears as a contained instrument and may
  become the final close. A page changes ground at most three times.
- No section becomes a centered title followed by equal cards. Punctuation,
  editorial split, interactive continuum and product instrument must alternate
  rhythm.
- Default-visible prose per act is normally 35-90 words and never exceeds 120
  words before an interaction or meaningful visual state. Metadata and labels
  do not count toward that budget.
- One detail state is open by default. Closed states show a name, not a second
  paragraph. The full accepted copy remains reachable.
- Long-form measure remains 560-620 px. No rendered type is below 12 px.
- Mobile is recomposed in reading order. Product state follows the statement;
  controls remain reachable without horizontal scrolling or sticky overlap.
