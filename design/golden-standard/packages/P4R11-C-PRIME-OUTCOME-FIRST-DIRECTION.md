## 1. Decision

The selected thesis is:

> **Outcome first. Judgment throughout. Execution as proof.**

The selected synthesis is:

- **C is the world and destination:** the finished AI-native company and the
  consequence of operating at machine speed;
- **B is the intelligence and behavior:** the page and its one product
  instrument respond meaningfully and expose mechanism;
- **A is the conscience:** warm human judgment, accountable ownership and
  editorial restraint keep the system legible and human.

This is role allocation inside one coherent Dagg world. It is not permission to
mix three unrelated visual styles.

## 2. Positioning

Dagg must read as an engineering and company-building force with strategic
intelligence - never as strategy consultants who learned enough technology to
sell an AI transformation.

The Home impression must be:

1. Dagg understands what AI makes possible at first-principles depth.
2. Dagg can decide how that capability should change a company.
3. Dagg can build the context, agents, software and controls that make the
   decision operational.
4. Human owners remain responsible for material judgment and exceptions.
5. The operating system learns from evidence and does not restart from zero.

The ambition principle is:

> **Nothing is too big in the future Dagg is willing to build. Nothing is
> unverified in the concrete claims Dagg makes today.**

The page may be bold, cinematic and consequence-led. It may not use hype,
borrowed scale, unsupported AGI claims or imitation defense theatre to create
that confidence.

## 3. Frozen argument

`design/golden-standard/narrative/HOME-NARRATIVE-FOUNDER-RESET.md` remains the
sole Home copy authority. The public story stays in this exact order:

1. `Build the company AI makes possible.`
2. `AI-native changes who executes - and who decides.`
3. `Start with how the company really works.`
4. `The decision becomes a working system.`
5. `Every operating cycle makes the next one better.`
6. `One partner, from direction to accountable release.`

All approved paragraphs, actions, links, boundaries and constructed-example
fields remain verbatim. Default-visible Home copy remains approximately 430-500
words, excluding global navigation and footer labels.

Composition must not solve a design problem by shortening the argument. The
argument must not solve a design problem by becoming a technical lecture.

## 4. C-Prime page experience

### Act 1 - Destination

- Desktop hero target: 96-100svh; stable Ink reading field and one authored
  Decision Field world.
- H1, lead and two actions are the only public content in the first viewport.
- The visual shows an AI-native operating landscape in a resolved state: human
  judgment has redirected imperfect company context into a governed, executable
  operating model.
- The image is environment, not sculpture. It must carry causal meaning without
  requiring a caption and must not resemble architecture, luxury materials,
  hardware, a spacecraft or a generic 3D AI render.
- No autoplay video. One finite arrival may resolve in 0.8-1.2 seconds and then
  hold. Reduced motion shows the resolved frame immediately.

### Act 2 - Strategic hinge

- Paper replaces Ink and creates a decisive change in rhythm.
- The operating-model shift is conclusion-led and readable as ordinary prose.
- No feature cards, icon row, dashboard or decorative secondary image.
- The section explains that the strategic decision is the division of work:
  preserve, simplify, automate, rebuild or retire, with named human
  responsibility where material uncertainty remains.

### Act 3 - Retained context

- WorkGraph appears by name only after the business consequence it serves is
  clear.
- The visual changes from atmosphere to inspectable structure. It shows
  different company-context sources becoming one retained record while source
  distinctions, owner and boundary remain visible.
- Decision Field stays primary; Operational Evidence is contained inside the
  instrument. Machine Signal does not enter this section.
- The reader must understand why the transformation does not restart at every
  phase.

### Act 4 - Governed execution

- Dagg Factory appears early enough to prevent the page from reading as a
  consultancy site.
- Home contains exactly one execution instrument. It is a bounded, inspectable
  Operational Evidence object, not a full-bleed photograph of an interface.
- The instrument exposes the exact four public states from the copy authority:
  operating reality, decision retained, governed execution and evidence
  returns.
- Each state is a visible diff. Every transition adds one distinct, nameable
  element:
  1. retained context record;
  2. explicit boundary plus accountable owner;
  3. permitted action inside the governed boundary;
  4. returned evidence plus preserved previous path.
- Captions support the states; they do not create differences that are absent
  in the visual.
- The customer-facing capability appears as one bounded consequence of the same
  governed system. It never becomes a generic Claude, ChatGPT, terminal or
  vendor-branded chat UI.

### Act 5 - Compounding consequence

- The outcome receives at least the visual weight of the execution instrument.
  It may not collapse into a small paragraph after the page has spent all of its
  energy on the artifact.
- The visual proves that evidence returns to the same retained record and
  sharpens the next decision and build.
- Machine Signal is permitted only as one finite evidence-return transition.
  It stops at a resolved, inspectable state.
- The section expresses machine-speed compounding without quantified claims.

### Act 6 - Accountability and next move

- The page returns to warm editorial confidence and closes decisively.
- Dagg's role spans strategic decision, build and accountable release.
- Human accountability must be visible through a specific owner marker,
  decision signature or named role in the execution proof. A stock portrait,
  anonymous human or team-size story is forbidden.
- The primary assessment action and the Company route remain explicit.
- The close does not add another abstract hero, feature grid or product summary.

## 5. Visual hierarchy

Home keeps the selected image-language allocation:

- full-page balance target: approximately 70% Decision Field, 25% Operational
  Evidence and 5% Machine Signal by narrative and visual role, not pixel count;
- hero: Decision Field primary, restrained Machine Signal support;
- WorkGraph: Decision Field with contained Operational Evidence;
- Factory proof: Operational Evidence primary; Machine Signal only as the
  finite state transition;
- consequence and close: Decision Field returns; no new image language.

No section uses all three roles. Operational Evidence is coded and inspectable,
not a generated-image world. Machine Signal never becomes wallpaper.

Role colors remain semantic:

- Coral: accountable human judgment or interruption;
- Sage: permission, boundary or governed stable state;
- Ink: machine precision and inspectable structure;
- Paper and Lift: retained context and human-readable explanation.

## 6. Page silhouette

At 1440 x 900 the complete Home target is approximately 4.8-5.3 viewports, not
the current C's roughly six-plus viewport scroll.

The silhouette must remain structurally distinct in grayscale:

1. full-viewport destination hero;
2. short, large Paper strategic hinge;
3. retained-context bridge that introduces one bounded instrument;
4. one wide dark execution chapter with finite state progression;
5. large warm consequence field;
6. concise editorial close.

The page may not repeat six equal left-copy/right-image rows. The dark surface
may occupy no more than roughly one third of the full page and is concentrated
in the hero and execution chapter.

## 7. Interaction thesis

The site itself must behave like an intelligent operating system. Interaction
reveals mechanism, consequence or constraint. It never exists to demonstrate
that animation is available.

The complete causal experience is:

1. show the resolved AI-native company;
2. expose the strategic division-of-work decision;
3. retain the operating context in WorkGraph;
4. carry the same context through Dagg Factory;
5. stop a proposed action at a visible human boundary;
6. return evidence to the record;
7. preserve the previous path and improve the next operating cycle.

The core story advances through normal scrolling. No click is required to
understand it. Optional pointer, focus and touch interaction may inspect states
or move between them.

## 8. Motion contract

Use no more than three meaningful motion moments on Home:

1. hero resolution: 0.8-1.2 seconds, once, then still;
2. instrument state progression: four finite diffs, with 220-300 ms state
   changes and no overshoot;
3. evidence return: one finite return and resolved hold.

The film may pin briefly on desktop but never lock, snap or hijack scroll.
Tablet and mobile remove sticky behavior when it compromises reading. Reduced
motion presents the resolved meaning immediately and keeps all labels available.
No-JavaScript presents the complete four states in semantic order.

## 9. Navigation and component behavior

The shared P4R10 navigation and component contract remains binding.

- Top-level labels are real links.
- Build remains a real overview link with a separate disclosure control.
- Desktop pointer and keyboard expose the same adaptive preview.
- Touch opens the reading sheet and restores focus when it closes.
- The outer flyout geometry stays stable while the bounded proof inset changes.
- Assessment remains visible and reachable in all navigation modes.
- Home hero and strategic acts use zero icons. The execution instrument uses at
  most two semantic marks if owner, boundary or evidence would otherwise be
  ambiguous.

## 10. Responsive composition

Desktop, iPad and mobile are authored modes, not scaled versions.

- Desktop: 12-column, 1240 px maximum field; 7/5 strategic and 45/55 product
  splits where appropriate.
- iPad landscape: bounded flyout and deliberate pointer-plus-touch behavior.
- iPad portrait: touch-first disclosure, recomposed hero and ordered execution
  states.
- Mobile: independently composed portrait hero; no desktop crop; no sticky
  film; all four execution states appear in document order; full-canvas
  navigation preserves focus containment and body lock.

Required browser verification widths remain those in P4R10 section 8.

## 11. C-Prime rejection tests

Any single failure below rejects the Figma target or coded route.

1. **Engineer-first test:** remove the Dagg name. If the page reads primarily as
   consulting, architecture, luxury materials or a design studio, reject it.
2. **Caption-strip test:** remove captions from the four execution states. If
   four different states are not visible, reject it.
3. **Image-blank test:** replace imagery with flat blocks. If no argument is
   lost, the imagery is decorative and its scale is unjustified.
4. **Copy-blank test:** remove body copy. If the visual cannot suggest governed
   execution with accountable ownership, it carries no narrative load.
5. **Owner test:** point to the visual owner and boundary. If the answer is only
   a sentence, reject it.
6. **Instrument test:** ask whether Act 4 shows a system or a picture of a
   system. `Picture` is a failure.
7. **Compounding test:** if Acts 5 and 6 disappear under a blur or squint while
   the hero dominates, the argument is inverted.
8. **Generic-AI test:** if the hero could plausibly belong to another AI company
   this year, reject it.
9. **Structural-difference test:** if A imagery can be swapped into C-Prime
   without changing the composition or behavior, C-Prime is not resolved.
10. **Restraint test:** reject any terminal, dashboard wallpaper, node map,
    circuit board, particle field, fake chat, biological form, sperm/ovum
    reading, defense theatre or endless loop.
11. **Capability test:** keyboard, touch, reduced motion and no-JavaScript must
    preserve the same conclusion, routes and accountable end state.
12. **Truth test:** reject any visual or copy that implies named client proof,
    managed operation, AGI capability, quantified productivity or production
    performance without approved evidence.
