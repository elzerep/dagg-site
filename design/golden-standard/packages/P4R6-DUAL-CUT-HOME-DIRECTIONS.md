# P4R6 - Dual-Cut Home Direction Gate

Status: **historical and superseded by P4R10; do not implement**  
Authority: Christian's 31 August dual-organization direction, the Golden
Standard Masterplan v1.2, the three approved design PDFs, P4R5, P4R5B and the image
production protocol  
Gate: three complete, jointly verified Home directions shown together

> The P4R6 routes are rejected diagnostic evidence. The current founder gate is
> the six-act Figma A/B/C comparison and immutable evidence defined by P4R10.

## 1. Outcome

Produce three genuinely different, complete Home vertical slices on one shared
Dagg design and interaction system:

- A - Strategic editorial;
- B - Product mechanism;
- C - Operational enterprise.

Each direction receives two independent internal first cuts: Codex and Claude.
The teams then cross-review each other's cut. The program/design director
creates one coherent `X1` from the strongest compatible decisions. Christian
sees only the three verified `A1`, `B1` and `C1` candidates.

These are three complete Home pages, not three full multi-page websites and not
three brands.

## 2. Organization

### Program and design direction - Codex

Owns product truth, positioning, site-level narrative, design-system fidelity,
image-generation lane, integration, acceptance and the final `X1` cut.

Codex specialist roles:

- positioning and narrative;
- copy and editorial density;
- visual orchestration and image-language mix;
- component, interaction and motion;
- executive/technical comprehension;
- adversarial peer and truth-boundary review.

### Build direction - Claude

Owns bounded browser implementation, interaction fidelity, responsive behavior,
performance evidence and a separate first-cut design interpretation.

Claude specialist roles:

- page and component architecture;
- interaction and accessibility;
- visual composition;
- responsive/performance verification;
- self-review before cross-review.

### Vision gate - Christian

Christian chooses only after `A1`, `B1` and `C1` pass joint operating
acceptance. He is not asked to approve internal cuts or implementation details.

## 3. Shared immutable inputs

Both organizations receive exactly the same:

1. current `CLAUDE.md` authority;
2. Golden Standard Masterplan v1.2;
3. approved Image Language System PDF;
4. approved Design System Foundations PDF;
5. approved Component and Interaction Library PDF;
6. passed P4R5 component library, binding P4R5B Component Intelligence

Neither `X0` implementation may inspect the other before its first cut freezes.
P4R6 cannot activate while any required slot is `unassigned`, any selected file
is absent from the registry, or a measured SHA-256 differs from the registry.

## 4. Shared Home truth

Every direction must make the whole company legible without a navigation click:

1. Home thesis and Dagg conclusion;
2. stakes - the operating gap compounds;
3. strategic transformation and the five decisions;
4. WorkGraph as retained company context;
5. Factory as the build mechanism;
6. one governed operating capability across two surfaces;
7. Operate / Trust - permission, ownership, escalation and way back;
8. Impact gateway - named evidence with provenance and an explicit truth
   boundary, without inventing outcomes;
9. assessment close - the next bounded decision.

Compounding learning is the connective behavior across acts 3-8, not a reason
to delete either the distinct Operate / Trust act or the Impact gateway.

WorkGraph is central but is not the whole proposition. Customer assistants are
access surfaces, not the source of truth. No direction may imply current managed
operations, unsupported client outcomes, model-provider endorsement or magical
autonomy.

## 5. Shared visual contract

- Import the exact passed P4R5 component CSS/JS; do not duplicate its tokens,
  header DOM, flyout behavior, controls or motion.
- Decision Field is the world, Operational Evidence is contained proof and
  Machine Signal is one finite transition.
- One section never uses all three.
- Home hero uses Decision Field plus restrained Machine Signal.
- Full-page target is the approved 70/25/5 image-language balance, interpreted
  by narrative area rather than raw pixel counting.
- No two image-heavy acts in sequence and at most three ground changes.
- All major assets are selected or generated through the shared Codex image
  lane from a frozen slot brief. Claude does not create a parallel image system.
- The P4R5 hero pair proves the shared carrier; it is not mandatory in A, B or
  C. Each direction must earn its own hero selection through its frozen slot
  manifest. Reuse is allowed only when the manifest records why it best serves
  that direction rather than merely being available.
- Image requests are blind and frozen: before either `X0` author sees a
  candidate, the slot manifest freezes the narrative job, visual family,
  dimensions, exclusion list and evaluation criteria. Codex may generate the
  shared candidates but cannot change that brief to privilege its own cut.
  Both builders receive the same candidate set, filenames and hashes at the
  same time. Selection decisions and rejected candidates are logged.
- Use real library icons; no inline SVG, CSS art, text symbols or emoji.

## 6. Direction A - Strategic editorial

Reader conclusion: `Dagg understands where the company must go and can make the
decision executable.`

Peer emphasis: Anthropic's warm editorial conviction plus McKinsey-level
strategic conclusion, proven with Dagg mechanisms.

Opening copy:

> Build the company AI makes possible.

> Dagg turns strategic direction into an AI-native operating model - then
> builds the context, agents and software that make it work.

Required rhythm:

- 7/5 editorial hero with a bounded Decision Field;
- stakes as a short typographic punctuation;
- strategic decision continuum before product detail;
- WorkGraph and Factory as concise proof acts;
- two surfaces as one contained Operational Evidence act;
- assessment as a restrained conclusion.

Copy budget: 390-500 visible words; hero at most 30 words beyond actions; first
two viewports at most 115 words; tone 70% strategic conclusion / 30% proof.

## 7. Direction B - Product mechanism

Reader conclusion: `Dagg has built a concrete transformation mechanism.`

Peer emphasis: xAI/OpenAI product clarity and discovery, translated into Dagg's
warm system and governed operating logic.

Opening copy:

> Turn company context into working systems.

> Dagg decides what should change, captures the operating logic in WorkGraph,
> and builds the agents or software that put it to work.

Required rhythm:

- immediate inspectable carrier;
- one persistent object across Context / Decision / Build / Permission /
  Action / Evidence;
- the decision continuum changes the same object's build plan;
- Factory is the dominant proof act;
- two surfaces change context, action, permission and evidence in the same
  carrier;
- sparse explanatory copy.

Copy budget: 270-380 visible words; hero at most 25 words; state text at most 12
words per state; tone 35% strategic framing / 65% inspectable mechanism.

## 8. Direction C - Operational enterprise

Reader conclusion: `Dagg can change a real enterprise operating model without
losing context, control or accountability.`

Peer emphasis: Palantir-level operational consequence, warmer, more human and
less militarized.

Opening copy:

> Change the operating model. Keep control.

> Dagg maps the real company, builds the justified agents and software, and
> keeps context, permissions, owners and evidence attached as work moves into
> execution.

Required rhythm:

- one concrete governed state in the opening;
- Intent / Boundary / Accountable owner / Way back remain visible;
- operational reality and exception before broad capability;
- WorkGraph carries provenance;
- Factory shows where execution stops and human judgment resumes;
- the customer surface preserves the same controls;
- evidence closes the loop.

Copy budget: 360-470 visible words; hero at most 32 words; operating-record
values at most 10 words; tone 45% enterprise consequence / 35% control and
proof / 20% strategic ambition.

## 10. Cross-review protocol

For each direction:

1. Freeze both `X0` snapshots and component-library hashes.
2. Confirm both cuts are eligible: the complete nine-act Home contract is
   present; all required slots resolve to the same frozen candidate bank; and
   viewport/state/evidence coverage is equal. An ineligible cut is repaired
   before comparison, never allowed to lose by missing evidence.
3. Claude reviews `X0-Codex`; Codex reviews `X0-Claude`.
4. Every finding names viewport, state, visible evidence, violated rule and
   severity.
5. Each review also names the three strongest transferable decisions.
6. Before synthesis, write a direction trade-off note stating what each cut
   optimizes, what it gives up, and which incompatibilities must remain choices.
   If the cuts reveal a genuine strategic fork, preserve both and make a clear
   director recommendation; do not force convergence merely to produce `X1`.
7. The director writes a synthesis brief selecting a narrative spine,
   composition, copy set, image orchestration and interaction model.
8. The `X1` builder implements that one brief. Do not average incompatible
   choices or concatenate both pages.
9. Codex and Claude jointly audit `X1` before the Christian gate.

## 11. Acceptance

Every `X1` must pass:

- same P4R5 component hashes;
- desktop at 1440 and 1280 px;
- iPad/tablet landscape at 1366 x 1024, 1180 x 820 and 1024 x 768;
- iPad/tablet portrait at 1024 x 1366, 820 x 1180 and 768 x 1024;
- the 940 px navigation boundary, plus mobile at 390, 360 and 320 px;
- tablet navigation and primary interactions work with touch alone, remain
  usable with a trackpad/keyboard, never depend on hover, and preserve the
  selected state across an orientation change;
- full keyboard, touch, reduced motion and fresh-load no-JS;
- no overflow, dead controls, remote requests, console errors or route errors;
- mobile first viewport contains thesis plus a meaningful part of its proof;
- copy budgets and truthful claims;
- correct image-language role per act and no decorative asset use;
- equal-viewport comparison with current peer evidence;
- a complete nine-act Home contract; compact writing cannot be used to omit a
  required business act;
- frozen asset-registry and slot-manifest hashes, equal candidate access and a
  recorded asset selection rationale;
- equal X0 eligibility and evidence coverage plus a written trade-off note and
  director recommendation;
- five-second, twenty-second and two-minute executive comprehension;
- no slide-deck rhythm, terminal wall, circuit aesthetic, bought-template feel,
  generic AI visual or reference pastiche;
- internal score at least 90/100 with no dimension below 8/10.

### A/B/C anti-convergence matrix

The final three must remain recognizably different after shared tokens, chrome
and imagery are removed from the comparison:

| Dimension | A - Strategic editorial | B - Product mechanism | C - Operational enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative spine | thesis -> strategic decision -> proof | persistent record -> state change -> result | operating reality -> boundary -> accountable outcome |
| Proof order | strategy before mechanism | mechanism in the opening | consequence and control before breadth |
| Primary carrier | bounded Decision Field | interactive operating record | governed decision / exception record |
| Interaction model | restrained editorial reveal | progressive, stateful discovery | inspectable boundary and escalation |
| First-reader conclusion | direction made executable | concrete transformation mechanism | change without lost control |

At least four of the five rows must remain different between every pair. If a
pair collapses to the same spine, proof order, carrier and interaction with only
copy or styling changed, both directions fail. The gate dossier includes this
matrix with visible evidence, the trade-off note and the director's honest
recommendation.

Only after all three pass are they opened together for Christian's direction
choice. Do not publish or deploy.
