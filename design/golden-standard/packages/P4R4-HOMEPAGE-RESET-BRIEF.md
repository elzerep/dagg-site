## 2. Christian's clarified image-system model

The approved portfolios are a generative grammar, not a fixed asset catalog.

- New stills are generated for the exact topic, text, narrative job, crop and interaction.
- Existing images are references and valid candidates only where their visible event fits the section.
- All three languages are valuable in the right role.
- The page must feel like one Dagg system, not three moodboards colliding.
- A Home page may contain moments from all three roles across the full composition, but one section never uses all three.

Frozen role hierarchy:

- Decision Field is the primary site world: strategy, transformation, judgment and company context.
- Operational Evidence is contained proof: WorkGraph, Factory, governance, retained records and operational states.
- Machine Signal is a restrained finite transition: speed, resolution and inherited learning. It is never a page world.

## 3. Home's job

Home is the executive summary of Dagg as a whole. It must make this sequence legible without turning into a method explainer:

1. the operating model is changing;
2. Dagg decides what the company should become and how work should change;
3. WorkGraph retains company-specific context and decisions;
4. Factory turns justified decisions into agents and software;
5. people set intent, requirements, material exceptions and verification;
6. retained context and operating evidence make the next intervention start ahead;
7. AI-native capability changes both internal operations and what the company can expose to its own customers;
8. the credible first step is a bounded assessment.

WorkGraph is central technology, not the whole company promise. Operational activity must not be claimed as a current managed-service proof.

## 5. Required correction

The next slice should use A's broad editorial architecture as scaffolding, not as accepted content. It may borrow B's sense of digital state change and product immediacy without adopting a dark page theme.

The Home hero needs a newly generated, story-specific Decision Field asset with a finite Machine Signal change. Operational Evidence must appear later as a separate coded, inspectable product mechanism. Generated dark material may frame it, but may not pretend to be a live Dagg product or client proof.
