## 1. Purpose

Review the Dagg site as one company expression, not as a collection of routes
or isolated components. The review must determine whether the current local
candidate makes design, narrative, technology, motion, navigation and copy say
the same thing:

> Dagg sees how an AI-native company should work, maps the operating reality,
> retains the company context, and builds governed agents and software that
> turn strategic direction into working systems.

The site must feel like an engineering-led company with strategic judgment,
not a strategy consultancy with a technical appendix. Its benchmark is the
discipline and confidence of Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI/SpaceXAI and Palantir,
translated into Dagg's own visual and product language.

This is a failure-first, independent review. Do not soften findings to agree
with Codex or the founder. Do not edit source, generate assets, assemble,
commit, deploy or publish.

## 2. Founder intent that must survive the whole site

1. Dagg helps existing companies transform and new companies start AI-native.
2. Strategy matters because the operating model changes before tooling does.
3. Dagg is execution-led: it can translate the decision into agents, custom
   software and governed operational systems.
4. WorkGraph is core technology, not a decorative knowledge graph. It retains
   workflows, evidence, decisions, owners, permissions, exceptions and domain
   context across transformation, build and operation.
5. Dagg Factory is core build capability. It carries requirements and retained
   context through product, architecture, implementation and evaluation.
6. Human judgment defines direction, requirements, permissions and material
   exceptions. Agents and software carry more execution.
7. The urgency is real: the gap between human-speed handoffs and machine-speed
   companies compounds. The voice remains calm and confident, never shrill.
8. Dagg does not advertise headcount or claim unsupported 10x/100x outcomes.
   Capability is demonstrated through mechanisms, judgment and working proof.
9. A company's own harness, context and permissions remain the source of
   truth. A frontier model or assistant is replaceable infrastructure or an
   interaction surface, not the owner of the customer's company intelligence.
10. No named client, operational result or outcome appears without evidence
    and permission.

## 6. Review dimensions

### A. Whole-site story and copy

Read the public site in two ways:

1. The global chapter spine: Home → Transformation → WorkGraph → Build → Trust
   → Company → Assessment.
2. Each route as a standalone chapter with a beginning, development, proof and
   handoff to the next relevant chapter.

Review every visible word: eyebrow, H1, lead, body copy, label, caption,
disclosure, form helper, status message, primary CTA, secondary CTA, header,
flyout, mobile menu and footer.

Flag:

- unexplained Dagg vocabulary;
- compressed engineering shorthand;
- consultancy language;
- generic AI claims or unsupported superiority;
- repeated propositions;
- a claim whose proof appears too late or not at all;
- a CTA whose label does not predict its destination;
- conflicting copy between contract, source and assembled HTML;
- a section that is beautiful but thin, or informative but exhausting.

### B. Visual system and image-language discipline

For every route, state its dominant image-language role and whether the actual
composition obeys it. The hierarchy remains:

- Decision Field surrounds and explains;
- Operational Evidence proves;
- Machine Signal transitions a causal state and stops.

No Hero may use more than one visual role. No section may use more than two.
The visual must be intelligible in roughly two seconds and support the nearby
copy without requiring interpretation.

Assess whether the site feels calm, warm, exclusive, intelligent and alive —
without becoming an art studio, a consultancy template, a generic AI site or a
technical terminal product.

### C. Components, navigation and interaction

Review the adaptive desktop flyout, mobile disclosure, buttons, links,
in-page navigation, disclosures, records, carriers, forms and feedback states.

The site should feel anticipatory and modern without becoming theatrical:

- hover or focus may preview; activation navigates;
- Escape closes and restores focus;
- touch uses explicit disclosure;
- background is inert when the mobile menu is open;
- no essential story depends on a click;
- scrolling may advance a finite story and must reverse where specified;
- optional controls remain available for inspection;
- no interaction exists only to decorate the page.

### D. Motion and resilience

Verify from source and any current evidence:

- Home, Transformation, WorkGraph and Factory reach their complete meaning
  without requiring a click;
- finite sequences stop;
- reduced motion resolves immediately to complete meaning;
- missing enhancement APIs resolve to complete meaning;
- no JavaScript preserves all public copy and causal states in document order;
- pause and replay controls are truthful and operable.

Do not claim rendered validation for a mode you did not actually observe.

### E. Responsive, accessibility and conversion

Assess desktop, iPad and mobile evidence separately. Flag squeezed desktop,
bad crops, clipped copy, weak reading order, invisible focus, small targets,
missing accessible names, false live-region announcements or lost context.

Follow the Assessment flow from every public `Start an assessment` action to
validation and its local-preview confirmation. No real inquiry may be sent.

### F. Technical and framework quality

Review whether the implementation architecture supports the intended quality:

- shared tokens and chrome rather than route drift;
- semantic HTML first, progressive enhancement second;
- deterministic, reversible or finite state logic;
- responsive media with intrinsic dimensions and authored crops;
- no avoidable layout shift;
- no raw oversized assets where optimized derivatives exist;
- stable canonical route strategy for eventual publication;
- no stale root site or preview-only routing ambiguity in the release plan;
- no console errors or broken anchors in the available evidence.

Do not recommend a fashionable framework merely because a peer uses it. A
framework change is justified only if it materially improves maintainability,
accessibility, motion control, performance or publishing reliability.
