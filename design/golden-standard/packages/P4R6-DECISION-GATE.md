# P4R6 decision gate — three complete homepage directions

Status: **superseded by P4R7 after Christian's rendered review on 1 September
2026. Do not use this file as an active decision gate.** The routes below are the
contract-required A1/B1/C1 syntheses produced after cross-review of both the
Codex and Claude X0 cuts. No production homepage is implied by this file and
nothing has been published.

The assembly checks recorded here remain historical evidence. The visible
quality conclusion does not: A1/B1/C1 were rejected as too long, too similar
and too information-dense, and A1 exposed a severe blank-state rendering
failure. The active brief is `P4R7-HOMEPAGE-CONVERGENCE.md`.

All three candidates use the same accepted Dagg foundations, image-language rules, shared adaptive chrome, component library, nine-act homepage narrative and factual boundaries. They differ in composition, emphasis, rhythm and the way the operating proof is introduced.

## A — Strategic editorial

- Route: `/preview/golden-standard/directions/a1/`
- Hero: `Build the company AI makes possible.`
- Image pair: A04 Decision Field with restrained Machine Signal.
- Primary impression: Anthropic warmth and editorial confidence, with the strategic decision placed before the product mechanism.
- Distinctive interaction: Preserve → Simplify → Automate → Rebuild → Retire decision continuum.
- Why it advances: it is the strongest expression of Dagg as the strategy-and-execution partner rather than a software vendor.

## B — Mechanism-led product

- Route: `/preview/golden-standard/directions/b1/`
- Hero: `Turn company context into working systems.`
- Image pair: B04 Decision Field with restrained Machine Signal and a visible coral judgment seam.
- Primary impression: product clarity, persistent operating record and an explicit state progression from context to evidence.
- Distinctive interaction: the same governed capability switches between the internal operating surface and the customer surface without losing its record, owner or permission boundary.
- Why it advances: it makes WorkGraph, Factory and the customer-facing capability feel like one compounding system.

## C — Governed execution

- Route: `/preview/golden-standard/directions/c1/`
- Hero: `Change the operating model. Keep control.`
- Image pair: C03 Decision Field with restrained Machine Signal; the coral accountability control sits inside transparent governance planes.
- Primary impression: enterprise authority, implementation capability and control at the point of action.
- Distinctive interaction: the operating record carries intent, boundary, accountable owner, way back and returned evidence through execution.
- Why it advances: it is the strongest answer to enterprise buyers who need to believe Dagg can execute inside consequential workflows.

## Shared acceptance result

Verified in the in-app browser:

- desktop 1440 × 900;
- iPad landscape 1024 × 768;
- iPad portrait 768 × 1024;
- mobile 390 × 844;
- small mobile 320 × 800;
- no horizontal overflow;
- nine acts in the required order;
- all selected images load in desktop and mobile art direction;
- no visible text below 11 px;
- no visible interactive target below 24 px in either inspected desktop or
  mobile states;
- no console warnings or errors in inspected states;
- adaptive desktop Build flyout opens on hover, changes its proof inset with
  the selected destination and closes with Escape;
- the mobile menu opens, locks the page, closes with Escape and restores focus;
- A1 decision, surfaces and governed-exception states respond;
- B1 decision changes the same Factory plan, and its surfaces and governed
  exception respond;
- C1 source tabs, decision, surfaces and permitted/material-exception states
  respond;
- every hero assessment CTA lands on the real shared assessment route with
  `Start with one material decision.` as its H1;
- generated outputs are deterministic and pass the shared assembly contract.

The resolved captures, equal-viewport peer comparison, interaction evidence
and reduced-motion limits are recorded in
`evidence/HOME-DIRECTIONS/resolved/QA.md`. A physical-device Enter/Space check
for native `<summary>` elements and an OS-level reduced-motion smoke test
remain pre-publication checks; neither changes the current A/B/C direction

## Gate question

Choose the primary worldview, not individual components:

- A if the first impression should be strategic judgment;
- B if the first impression should be the compounding product mechanism;
- C if the first impression should be governed execution and control.

The chosen direction becomes the homepage master. Strong secondary moments from the other two may be incorporated only when they preserve the chosen hierarchy and image-language ratios.
