## 1. Why P4R2 exists

Christian rejected P4 as the only direction presented for approval. The copy
was too long and abstract, Operational Evidence dominated the page, and the
result felt more like terminal-heavy slides than a living Dagg website.

P4R2 attempted to restore the intended gate through three generated full-page
images. Christian rejected all three on 31 August 2026. The images turned the
image language into the page architecture, produced oversized art-led fields,
and lost Dagg's strategic + AI/product character. This was a method failure,
not a request to select the least-bad image.

The next gate must therefore use three real browser slices. HTML, typography,
spacing, navigation, product behavior and motion are authored as web. Image
generation is limited to bounded assets with a declared slot and semantic role.

## 2. The visual language is binding in every concept

The three concepts vary narrative and composition inside one Dagg system:

- **Decision Field · approximately 70% of governed image-language moments.**
  Warm editorial material, strategic judgment and human intervention.
- **Operational Evidence · approximately 25% of governed image-language
  moments.** Contained product proofs for WorkGraph, Factory, governance or an
  operating state.
- **Machine Signal · approximately 5% of governed image-language moments.** A
  finite, meaning-bearing transition showing resolution, inherited context or
  velocity. It is motion behavior, never a third page theme.

These are not viewport-area targets, section-height targets or permission to
make generated art occupy most of a page. They govern which visual language a
moment belongs to. Page composition remains governed by the measured peer
template: one ground, sparse and concentrated media, asymmetric grid, varied
section heights, short copy and inspectable product proof.

One section has one primary job: `world`, `instrument` or `transition`. A
section never mixes all three. A surrounds and explains; C proves; B connects
states.

The approved section map is exact. `Decision Field + Machine Signal` in the
Home hero means a bounded conceptual/mechanism element whose state resolves; it
does not mean a full-bleed generated image. Operational Evidence may appear in
the same viewport or immediately after it as a separate contained instrument.

| Section | Primary | Support | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home hero | Decision Field | Machine Signal | Judgment redirects a system toward an operational state. |
| Transformation | Decision Field | None | Strategy changes work, not just tooling. |
| WorkGraph | Decision Field | Operational Evidence | Context becomes a retained, traversable company record. |
| Build | Operational Evidence | Machine Signal | Requirements resolve into governed, verified software and agents. |
| Impact | Operational Evidence | Decision Field | A named result has provenance, consequence and a truth boundary. |
| Company | Decision Field | None | Dagg's ambition and judgment are felt without a headcount story. |

Operational Evidence may not appear as the Home hero's supporting mode.

## 3. Three independent narrative hypotheses

### The Operating Shift

**Commercial lens:** strategic transformation.  
**Hero:** `The operating model is changing.`  
**Body:** `Dagg redesigns how companies work — and builds the agents, software
and operating systems that make the change real.`  
**Primary proof:** one decision moving from operating-model judgment into a
bounded intervention.  
**Editorial follow-through:** `Strategy, translated into execution.`

### Proof in Motion

**Commercial lens:** execution capability.  
**Hero:** `Strategy that changes how the company runs.`  
**Body:** `Dagg carries one company-specific context from decision to agents,
software and governed operation.`  
**Primary proof:** a light, inspectable WorkGraph-to-Factory state transition;
never a terminal or dashboard wall.  
**Editorial follow-through:** `A decision becomes a working system.`

### The Compounding Gap

**Commercial lens:** the stakes of machine speed.  
**Hero:** `Build for machine speed.`  
**Body:** `The gap between human-speed and AI-native companies compounds. Dagg
makes the transition operational.`  
**Primary proof:** a bounded company path resolving from reconstructed handoffs
into governed machine-speed execution.  
**Editorial follow-through:** `The transition is not a tool rollout.`

All three use the accepted navigation and `Start an assessment` primary CTA.
None may mention headcount, productivity multiples, unapproved customers,
invented outcomes or a guaranteed transformation result.

## 4. Copy restraint gate

Before Christian sees a concept:

- H1 is at most 10 words and carries one thought.
- Hero body is at most 26 words and contains no list of Dagg capabilities.
- Default-visible first viewport contains at most 55 words excluding chrome,
  provenance and controls.
- An H2 is at most 12 words.
- A section intro is at most 32 words.
- No paragraph explains what an adjacent image or interaction already shows.
- Abstract nouns may not stack more than two deep.
- The page demonstrates capability before it describes methodology.

The Foundations type contract remains binding: Geist carries headings,
navigation, actions and interface labels; Newsreader carries leads,
explanation, captions and selective judgment; JetBrains Mono is reserved for
real metadata. It never becomes body copy or decorative code.

## 5. Visual and product-proof gate

Before Christian sees a concept:

- warm paper is the reading ground; Decision Field is a bounded semantic
  moment, not the page background or a required full-viewport image;
- media is sparse and concentrated. A slice does not add one conceptual image
  per section, and image coverage is never used as a proxy for brand strength;
- no more than two Operational Evidence instruments are present;
- no instrument resembles a terminal, console, log wall or generic dashboard;
- no black table occupies more than one quarter of a desktop viewport;
- coral marks a material human decision, sage a governed boundary and ink
  machine precision;
- WorkGraph is central but does not become the entire company story;
- Factory proves execution rather than listing a software pipeline;
- Machine Signal has one causal before/after state and a resolved reduced-motion
  state;
- no circuit board, node cloud, neon AI, brain, stock human, generated workshop
  scene or invented client proof appears.

Foundations adds these measurable constraints:

- Paper 55-60%, Lift 15-20%, Ink 15-20%, Coral 4-6% and Sage 2-4% at page level;
- at most three page-ground changes;
- Ink is contained inside mechanisms, with full bleed reserved for a decisive
  act or final close;
- Coral marks a choice, intervention or accessible action and never becomes a
  decorative stripe;
- 1240px maximum field, twelve columns, 7/5 strategic split, 45/55 product
  split and 940px collapse;
- mobile is recomposed while semantic reading order remains identical;
- section rhythm varies by argument; repeated global padding that creates a
  slide deck fails;
- sections have no radius; controls use 4px, editorial surfaces 10px and
  instruments 14px;
- actions are at least 44x44px, mobile navigation controls 48px and focus is a
  2px ring with 2px offset;
- no autoplay video appears in the initial viewport;
- normal motion uses finite micro/response timing without overshoot; reduced
  motion snaps position and size while preserving labels and resolved meaning.

## 6. Required replacement output

Each of the three replacements is a real, responsive browser slice, not a
generated page image. Each must show:

1. accepted Dagg chrome;
2. complete hero copy and both hero actions;
3. one bounded Decision Field moment integrated with copy or mechanism;
4. one editorial act establishing the concept's thesis;
5. one concise, non-terminal product proof;
6. the start of the next act so the page rhythm is visible;
7. motion intent and the semantic roles of coral, sage and ink.

The three browser slices must differ in hierarchy, layout strategy, interaction model
and commercial framing. Reusing one composition with new copy fails the gate.

## 7. Stop condition

After exactly three browser slices are visible and independently pre-audited,
stop. Do not merge one direction until Christian selects, revises or combines
them.

P4R3's pre-slice component and interaction contract is binding before any
browser direction is built. The three slices may compose and stress-test those
primitives but may not invent a parallel component system. After Christian
selects and accepts a production slice, the verified implementation is
reconciled into the final Component and Interaction Library so documentation,
behavior and code cannot diverge.
