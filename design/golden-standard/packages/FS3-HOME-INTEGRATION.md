## 1. Objective

Rebuild Home as the concise, complete Dagg company story: strategic direction
opens the page; operating reality grounds the decision; WorkGraph retains the
context; Dagg Factory turns it into a governed system; operation and evidence
compound; Dagg remains accountable from direction through release.

Home must feel like an intelligent engineering company with strategic judgment,
not a strategy consultancy with a technical appendix. It must remain calm,
legible and premium without becoming passive, thin or generically minimal.

## 4. Binding narrative and visual composition

### Act 1 — Position

- One scenographic Hero field, not a right-hand card.
- Use one calm, immediately legible **Decision Field** image language only.
- Do not place Machine Signal, Operational Evidence, a dashboard, terminal,
  composite system diagram or second image in the Hero.
- Copy remains clearly readable on every crop. Never place live text inside the
  raster image.
- The Hero must be visibly present; negative stacking may not place it behind
  the page ground.

The image and copy work together. The image is not required to explain the
whole proposition alone. Its two-second job is to show imperfect operating
reality passing through an act of judgment into coherent, buildable order.

### Act 2 — Operating shift

- Text-led Paper passage with an assertive editorial composition.
- No raster image and no decorative interface.
- Use proportion and pacing, not a slide-like two-column explainer.

### Act 3 — Map and retain

- WorkGraph is named only after the business consequence is clear.
- Use a compact retained-context composition derived from the accepted FS2
  WorkGraph invariants, not the full deep-route carrier.
- Show one identity and continuity, not a feature grid.
- The record remains illustrative and must retain its disclosure.

### Act 4 — Build and expose the capability

- Dagg Factory carries the same record forward. Do not create symmetric
  WorkGraph and Factory marketing cards.
- One connected execution artifact shows four states:
  operating reality, decision retained, governed execution, evidence returns.
- Use a separate, restrained **Machine Signal** passage to express resolution,
  velocity and intelligence. It is not a Hero composite and not ambient
  wallpaper.
- Scroll entry starts one finite 0.8–1.2 second causal transition. It stops,
  holds and never loops by itself.
- No click is required. Pause and Replay may exist as optional controls only.
- The customer-facing capability remains one governed product proof moment,
  never a fake ChatGPT, Claude or provider-branded chat interface.

### Act 5 — Operate, measure and compound

- Return to a calm Paper or Lift ground.
- No new visual language or extra dashboard.
- The same record and returned evidence carry the meaning.

### Act 6 — Partner and next move

- Make the combination of strategic judgment, company building and engineering
  explicit without a headcount story.
- One strong assessment action and one secondary accountability route.
- The footer supplies the final Ink ground; do not add another heavy stage.

Across Home, use no more than three meaningful ground changes and one focal
system in motion at a time.

## 5. Interaction contract

1. A visitor who only scrolls understands the complete story.
2. Motion is finite, causal and user-respecting; it never becomes an endless
   proof of "AI-ness".
3. Click remains for navigation, CTA, optional inspection, Pause and Replay.
4. On desktop, tablet and mobile, core meaning is visible without hover.
5. Reduced motion and missing `IntersectionObserver` show the resolved state
   immediately.
6. With JavaScript disabled, all public sentences and all four execution states
   remain in semantic reading order.
7. Mobile and iPad use recomposed linear reading, not a squeezed desktop scene
   or tall sticky mechanism.

## 6. Density and pacing

- Default-visible Home copy: 430–500 words.
- First viewport: 35–55 words, H1, lead and two actions only. Do not add filler
  to hit the band; visual presence and the next-section edge carry depth.
- The page must not repeat the five outcomes, full Trust framework, full
  WorkGraph fields or complete Factory architecture.
- Target a materially shorter mobile reading experience than the current
  approximately nine-viewports implementation. Compress spacing and duplicated
  interface metadata, not the argument.
- Progressive copy may add at most 100 words and must not hide a causal step.
