# P4R5B — Component Intelligence Revision

Status: binding revision for P4R6 A/B/C synthesis  
Date: 1 September 2026  
Supersedes: P4R5 only where this document is more specific about icon use,
adaptive behavior and documentation quality  

## 1. Why this revision exists

The P4R5 component substrate passed its functional gate: 40 of 40 measured
checks across pointer, keyboard, mobile, no-JavaScript and reduced-motion
states. That pass remains valid.

The first Component and Interaction Library PDF did not pass visual
documentation review. It used the wrong page format, several screenshots were
captured at incomplete scroll positions and Component 07 appeared cropped. It
also documented utility icons without stating a sufficiently strict public-site
icon budget.

This revision closes those gaps without reopening the accepted foundations or
forking the component substrate.

## 2. Peer-backed conclusion

Fresh official-page evidence is stored in
`evidence/COMPONENT-LIBRARY-AUDIT/2026-08-31/`.

- Anthropic derives identity mainly from typography, editorial material,
  imagery and product-world behavior. Primary navigation remains mostly text.
- xAI uses a very small set of simple product marks beside stateful, product-
  near evidence. The mark does not carry the claim by itself.
- OpenAI uses familiar utility symbols and more product/tool icons inside real
  platform mechanisms, while marketing hierarchy remains type- and media-led.
- The strongest shared principle is therefore not icon abundance. It is a
  small utility layer plus distinct semantic identity only where a product
  concept must be made legible.

## 3. Binding public-site icon budget

The internal library is a capability, not a usage quota.

- Home hero: zero icons.
- Editorial or strategic section: zero icons by default.
- Product instrument: normally zero to two semantic icons in the visible
  state, only when they clarify state, ownership, boundary or evidence.
- Global navigation: familiar utility icons only where behavior needs them,
  such as disclosure, close or external destination. Do not place an icon
  before every top-level label.
- Feature or capability grids may not use interchangeable icons as their main
  differentiator.
- Never add an icon because a layout feels empty.

Images, typography, spacing, motion and concrete WorkGraph or Factory states
carry the Dagg narrative. Icons remain subordinate.

## 4. Two-layer icon architecture

### Layer 01 — utility

Use the licensed vendored Lucide subset for menu, close, disclosure, external
destination, play, pause and replay. A critical control retains a visible label
or an accessible name. Utility icons are visually neutral and never become
brand theatre.

### Layer 02 — Dagg semantic concepts

The semantic system currently maps licensed source pictograms into a Dagg-owned
carrier, concept, tone, state and motion grammar. The eight concepts are:

1. Decision — direction chosen;
2. Context — company record retained;
3. Boundary — authority resolved;
4. Build — system composed;
5. Verification — standard passed or held;
6. Evidence — claim grounded;
7. Ownership — person accountable;
8. Way back — reversal remains explicit.

Coral denotes material human judgment. Sage denotes governed resolution. Ink
denotes machine structure. Lift denotes retained context. The source pictogram
may later be replaced by a proprietary vector set without changing the concept
or component contract.

The larger semantic collection is useful for product UI, presentations and
future documentation. It is not permission to expose all eight concepts on a
marketing page.

## 5. Adaptive intelligence contract

Modernity must be felt as a meaningful response to reader intent.

### Global navigation

- Build opens after 70–100 ms of pointer intent and closes after 180–240 ms of
  grace.
- Four destinations change one proof inset: overview, Factory, agents and
  software, trust and operation.
- The outer panel remains stable. Content may crossfade in 100–140 ms and the
  inset may morph within 180–240 ms without layout jump.
- Pointer and keyboard reveal equivalent states.
- Touch uses one-at-a-time disclosure. No critical path depends on hover.
- Every destination remains a real link and preserves a useful context into
  the destination page.

### Product instruments

- An action changes context, recommendation or action, permission and evidence
  together when those fields belong to the same capability.
- A transition has one focal change, a named end state and a truth boundary.
- Idle animation, endless particle motion and decorative terminal output are
  forbidden.
- Reduced motion resolves immediately to the same semantic state.
- No-JavaScript retains the complete reading path and removes inert enhanced
  controls.

## 6. Image-system relationship

The component library never replaces the approved image system.

- Decision Field surrounds and explains.
- Operational Evidence proves through a contained coded mechanism.
- Machine Signal may connect two states and then stops.
- A section uses at most two image-language roles.
- Icons may annotate a real instrument but may not become a fourth image
  language.

## 7. Responsive and accessibility truth

Every A/B/C direction must verify desktop, iPad landscape, iPad portrait and
mobile. Pointer, keyboard and touch are separate proofs. Minimum targets, focus,
contrast, zoom, no-JavaScript and reduced-motion rules from P4R5 remain binding.
