# P4R4 - Christian authoritative addendum

Status: binding for the current Home slice and subsequent page system  
Date: 31 August 2026  
Purpose: preserve the full Dagg thesis while the implementation is simplified

## 1. The benchmark is a structural reference, not a visual collage

Re-verify a meaningful sample of Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI/SpaceX and Palantir
before accepting the slice. Do not invent a web structure merely to appear
original. Translate the strongest proven pattern from each peer into Dagg:

- Anthropic: warm editorial restraint, human judgment, conceptual imagery and
  intellectual calm;
- OpenAI: sparse mission and product clarity, with the product allowed to speak
  before explanatory copy;
- xAI/SpaceX: premium technical energy, modern interaction and responsive
  navigation/flyouts that make the company feel current;
- Palantir: company-specific context, trust boundaries, governance and concrete
  operational proof.

McKinsey is a tone and reasoning reference, not the visual benchmark: conclusion
first, strategic confidence and little need to advertise the confidence.

Dagg must feel like one coherent company at the intersection of those strengths,
not a page that alternates between four borrowed styles.

## 2. The Dagg synthesis

Dagg combines strategic judgment, company-building experience and technical
execution. The site must make the sequence legible without explaining every
internal detail:

1. understand where the company and its market are going;
2. map how the company actually works and decide what should change;
3. preserve reusable company context, rules, evidence and permissions in the
   WorkGraph and the broader customer harness;
4. build the justified agents or software through Factory;
5. govern the result, learn from operation and carry that evidence forward.

The visitor should infer execution capability from the mechanisms and evidence.
Do not compensate with self-congratulation, 10x claims or a long capability list.

## 3. Two inseparable AI-native surfaces

The complete site must show both surfaces as consequences of the same governed
company capability. FS5 establishes the internal surface as the primary promise
and carries the external customer-edge proposition as exact Build copy only.
The stateful two-surface proof is deferred deliberately to FS6; this prevents a
generic chat mock from being inserted merely to satisfy the concept before its
interaction, routing and evidence boundary are production-ready.

### Inside the company

Work moves from human-speed reconstruction toward machine-speed execution.
Agents can execute continuously inside explicit context, permissions and
evaluation. People retain direction, requirements, material exceptions and
verification.

### At the customer edge

The company's product is no longer confined to its own interface. Governed
capabilities can meet the customer where the customer already works, including
through an MCP integration surfaced in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. This is not a
generic chat product and must not be rendered as a fake vendor-branded chat UI.

FS6 must use one elegant product-proof interaction: a natural-language request
resolves against company-specific context, produces a recommendation or bounded
action, crosses an explicit permission boundary and returns evidence. The
customer surface is an interface; WorkGraph, Factory and the customer's harness
remain the governed source of context, rules, data, permissions and actions.
Use the earlier P4R5 two-surface component contract as the starting authority;
do not invent a parallel interaction grammar.

The Salesforce-Anthropic Agentforce/Claude integration is a useful market
pattern for this second surface. Do not call it "ClaudeForce" and do not imply
that Dagg depends on Salesforce, Claude or any single frontier model.

## 4. Homepage narrative test

Home remains the executive summary of the whole company. The visible sequence
must answer, with one conclusion and one proof moment per section:

1. Why must a company change now?
2. What operating decision should it make?
3. How does company context survive and become reusable?
4. How does the decision become agents or software?
5. How does the same governed capability change the company internally and the
   product externally?
6. Why does the next intervention begin ahead instead of from zero?
7. How does a serious customer begin?

Detail pages deepen this story; they do not repair a Home page that failed to
communicate it.

## 5. Interaction and visual translation

- Build a premium desktop flyout with hover intent, focus, Escape, close grace
  and a distinct mobile disclosure. It should feel deliberate and spatially
  responsive, never like a plus-sign utility control or a purchased template.
- The warm editorial world is primary. Dark operational instruments are
  contained proof. Finite machine motion resolves a causal state and stops.
- Product mechanisms must be inspectable and topic-specific. Do not use terminal
  walls, circuit-board graphs, generic dashboards or decorative AI motion.
- Decision Field images are a generative grammar. Create a composition for the
  exact thesis and crop; never reuse a moodboard image because it is available.
- In FS5, the internal and customer-edge relationship is copy-only on Build; no
  extra stateful carrier is authorized. In FS6, the two modes occupy one
  governed carrier so the reader understands that they are two surfaces of the
  same capability.

## 6. Acceptance standard

Codex and Claude both self-review before Christian sees the slice. Each must
compare the same rendered states against fresh peer evidence and the approved
image/design-system documents. Machine tests do not overrule a visible failure.

Reject the slice if it reads as a design studio, consultant deck, terminal,
circuit board, generic AI template or unsubstantiated manifesto. Accept it only
when the page conveys strategic judgment, product intelligence, operational
credibility and premium modern interaction as one Dagg system.
