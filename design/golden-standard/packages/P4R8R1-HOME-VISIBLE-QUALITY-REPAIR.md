## 4. Required repairs

### A. Beat 3 must contain the promised artifacts

The two provenance lines currently qualify plain prose. Implement the two
related coded artifacts required by `HOME-IMAGE-AND-NARRATIVE-MAP.md`:

1. one representative WorkGraph record with a small, legible set of real
   semantic fields such as request/workflow, approved decision, owner, boundary
   and source/provenance;
2. one illustrative Factory build plan in which the same approved intervention
   appears with evaluation, permission and way-back criteria.

Use accessible HTML and realistic synthetic data. These are semantic product
components, not CSS/vector art, terminal windows, dashboards, generated images
or two generic marketing cards. Keep each existing proposition, provenance line
and route adjacent to its artifact.

Do not invent a customer, deployment, outcome, assurance or new marketing
claim. Interface field labels may use the vocabulary already defined by the
content contract and Home maps.

### B. The proof must show the complete causal argument

In the default rendered reading path, make both the strategic decision and the
accountable result legible without requiring a click. The selected state may
still expose detail, but the component may not hide:

- the service owner as accountable owner;
- the material ownership boundary that withholds commitment;
- the return of decision and evidence to WorkGraph;
- the previous queue as the way back.

Reduce the empty dark area. The stage must be content-sized and inspectable,
not a 1180 x 620 black box carrying only two lines. Preserve one stable record
identity and one coherent instrument.

Sage must appear when the permission boundary becomes operative and remain in
the resolved state. Use it as a graphic boundary/state cue, not low-contrast
body text. Coral remains the single accountable judgment marker.

### C. Strengthen the first-screen composition

Keep the approved hero pair and still-only V1 treatment. Increase its delivered
authority so it does not read as a small supporting thumbnail. At 1280 x 720,
target an image carrier of roughly 540-610 px width while preserving:

- two H1 lines;
- the full lead and two actions;
- all protected image events;
- the accepted first-screen word burden;
- a calm, non-crowded composition.

Use a justified 45/55 or equivalent asymmetry; do not crop, frame as a card or
make the image a full-bleed blaffa.

### D. Compose the typographic acts

Beat 2 and Beat 5 currently read as ordinary left-flow text surrounded by
unused space. Give them deliberate editorial asymmetry using the accepted grid:

- Beat 2: conclusion and explanation occupy distinct but connected grid roles,
  with one structural rule or alignment anchor; no image or decorative object.
- Beat 5: use the accepted 5/7 close so the thesis and supporting body/actions
  visibly relate rather than forming a centered generic CTA block.

Whitespace must establish hierarchy around earned objects, not become an empty
half-page.

### E. Restore color semantics

Coral may mark accessible actions and the single accountable judgment. It may
not color generic eyebrows, `PROVENANCE` keys or section-category labels.
Eyebrows and metadata keys use Ink 2 / the accepted metadata role. Sage remains
the governance/boundary cue.
