## What Home must do

Home is the concise company story. It must let an intelligent reader understand
the complete Dagg arc without already knowing Dagg vocabulary:

`strategic direction -> operating reality -> decision -> retained company context -> build -> governed operation -> measured learning`

The page is not a product manual and it is not a consulting manifesto. It gives
each part enough ordinary-language explanation to make the next part inevitable.
The deeper routes then open the relevant chapter.

The same exact narrative must be used in all three Home design directions. A, B
and C may change composition, scale, motion, interaction and visual emphasis;
they may not solve a weak layout by changing the argument.

## Headline spine

1. Build the company AI makes possible.
2. AI-native changes who executes — and who decides.
3. Start with how the company really works.
4. The decision becomes a working system.
5. Every operating cycle makes the next one better.
6. One partner, from direction to accountable release.

Read alone, the sequence moves from possibility to consequence, method,
technology, execution and continuity. The paragraphs below make the sequence
clear; the headings are not expected to carry the whole explanation.

## Act 2 — The operating shift

**H2**  
AI-native changes who executes — and who decides.

**Body**  
The decisive shift is from people carrying every handoff to people setting
direction, defining requirements and resolving material exceptions while agents
and software execute continuously. The gap between human-speed handoffs and
machine-speed systems compounds.

Automation is not the answer to every process. The first decision is what to
preserve, simplify, automate, rebuild or retire — and where a person must stay
responsible.

## Density and presentation contract

- The public narrative above is the source. Interface labels, captions and
  evidence fields support it; they do not replace its paragraphs.
- Default-visible Home copy must remain inside the hard 420-520 word band; no
  more than 500 is the target, not an activation ceiling. The count includes
  headings, actions, captions and the connected execution artifact. Global
  navigation and footer labels are counted separately.
- Internal narrative handoff questions never render and never enter the word
  count. They exist only to test causality between acts.
- The first viewport carries the H1, lead and two actions only. It does not try
  to explain WorkGraph or Dagg Factory before the operating shift is clear.
- WorkGraph and Dagg Factory appear as named capabilities inside one connected
  story, not as isolated feature cards.
- Home uses one concrete execution moment. It should show context becoming a
  permitted action, an exception reaching a human owner and evidence returning.
- The page closes after the partner and assessment move. It does not repeat the
  five outcomes, the full Trust framework or the deep product architecture.

## Rejection criteria

Reject a Home direction if:

- the visual can be understood without reading the story but the story cannot be
  understood without decoding the visual;
- the copy reads like labels, bullets or record fields instead of an argument;
- WorkGraph or Dagg Factory appears before the business consequence it serves;
- the operating and measurement story is omitted or deferred as a future idea;
- the external customer-interface proof becomes a generic ChatGPT or Claude UI;
- every act receives equal visual weight and the page becomes a catalogue;
- urgency becomes a threat, or ambition becomes an unsupported claim;
- A, B and C use different copy to disguise that the layouts are not genuinely
  different.
