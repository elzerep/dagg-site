Transformation is a conclusion-led editorial argument with one inspectable
strategic decision. It is not a consulting deck, a process-diagram gallery or a
technology page with strategy copy placed above it.

The page has seven semantic sections but only four visual acts:

1. **Operating thesis** - Transformation 1 and 2 share one warm opening world.
2. **Operating reality to decision** - Transformation 3 and 4 form one
   continuous decision instrument.
3. **Transition and horizon** - Transformation 5 and 6 explain how the company
   changes while remaining governable.
4. **Bounded next move** - Transformation 7 closes the argument.

The semantic order remains exactly:

`Thesis -> Division of work -> Map -> Decision -> Roadmap -> Horizon -> Next move`

The visual system is deliberately narrower than Home:

- **Primary and only page image family: Decision Field.**
- **Supporting image family: none.** Transformation does not use Operational
  Evidence imagery or Machine Signal imagery.
- The page may contain one coded strategic decision instrument. A semantic
  component is not a license to introduce a second image family.
- The V2 hero remains the accepted semantic state, but its large field may use
  one finite 1.0-1.5 s editorial settle on load. It never loops, reveals new
  meaning, scans, parallax-shifts or becomes Machine Signal.
- The only custom stateful enhancement is the five-outcome decision continuum.
  Map and roadmap depth may use honest native disclosures; they do not become
  additional interactive spectacles.

The image-language production test is exact: **strategy changes work, not just
tooling.** A visual that merely makes fragments look tidier fails.

## 6. Transformation 2 - A different division of work

### Section job

Explain AI-native as a changed division of work rather than a faster tool. This
is the page's principal strategic reasoning and must read as connected prose,
not four marketing bullets.

The next question is: **Where does the current operating reality differ from
that division?**

### Exact accepted copy

**H2**  
Machine speed requires a different division of work.

**Body**  
An AI-native company does not merely give every employee a faster tool. It
changes where execution sits. Agents and software can carry stable paths
continuously; people set direction, define requirements, resolve material
exceptions and verify what the system produces.

**Body**  
That division is never absolute. Judgment stays where consequence, ambiguity
or accountability makes it valuable. The operating model must say where the
machine may act, where it must stop and who owns the decision when it does.

**Object labels**

- Agents - continuous execution within explicit permissions.
- Software - durable workflow, interfaces, records and controls.
- Owners - accountability for the result after release.

## 8. Transformation 4 - Choose the intervention

### Section job

Turn the map into the page's one inspectable strategic decision. Prove that a
build is one outcome, not Dagg's premise.

The next question is: **How can the chosen intervention begin without forcing
the company into a large programme?**

### Exact accepted copy

**Anchor**  
`decide`

**Eyebrow**  
The strategic decision

**H2**  
The first decision is what not to automate.

**Intro**  
Every mapped path is tested against five outcomes. Building is one possible
result, never the premise.

**Preserve**  
Keep the work as it is when the effort is proportionate to its value and human
judgment is the point of the work.

**Simplify**  
Remove steps, approvals or transfers that no longer protect a material outcome
before encoding the process in software.

**Automate**  
Let agents execute a stable path when inputs, rules, exceptions and success
criteria can be made explicit.

**Rebuild**  
Redesign the workflow and its software together when the current system
preserves the wrong operating model.

**Retire**  
Stop doing work that no longer changes a decision, protects a requirement or
serves the customer.

**Closing line**  
A recommendation is not complete until it names an owner, a boundary and the
evidence that would change it.

### Component assignment

`DecisionContinuum`

- Reuse Component 05's accepted one-axis behavior, not its old readout copy.
- All five outcome labels remain visible in this exact order:
  `Preserve -> Simplify -> Automate -> Rebuild -> Retire`.
- The endpoints communicate keep/stop through ordinary text and structure,
  never through a coral stripe, icon row or diagram.
- `Preserve` is the initial selected state because it most clearly proves that
  a build is not the premise.
- One selected readout shows the exact accepted explanation. The other four
  explanations are progressive sibling states. The closing line remains
  visible and stable beneath the readout.
- Use one contained Surface with a 10 px radius only for the current readout.
  The section itself has no radius. The five stations do not become five cards.
- Coral marks only the selected strategic judgment. Sage may mark the stable
  owner/boundary conclusion; neither color carries state alone.
- No icons are used.

### Interaction contract

- Use a semantic fieldset with five radio controls or an equivalent
  single-selection group. It is not navigation and does not use tab semantics.
- Pointer, keyboard and touch select the same state. Arrow-key behavior follows
  the chosen native control pattern.
- Targets are at least 44 x 44 px and at least 48 x 48 px for the touch-first
  composition.
- Selection uses the 220-300 ms state-change token, no overshoot and no carrier
  height jump. This is ordinary component response, not Machine Signal.
- The selected outcome and its readout are exposed programmatically. A polite
  announcement may name the selected outcome but may not repeat the entire
  paragraph.
- At desktop composition with a fine pointer, no reduced motion and a visible
  document, the continuum may run one finite preview after it has remained at
  least 60% visible for 800 ms: Preserve -> Simplify -> Automate -> Rebuild ->
  Retire -> Preserve. Each outcome holds 2200 ms; Retire holds 1000 ms. It
  never loops or restarts after a reader selection.
- Pointer presence pauses only. Focus pauses until an explicit Resume preview.
  Any direct outcome selection permanently wins for the page view. Automatic
  state changes are silent; manual selection may announce only the outcome.
- Tablet, touch/coarse-pointer, reduced-motion and no-JavaScript compositions
  remain manual-only. No fake typing, progress fill, carousel or hover-only
  explanation is introduced.
- Reduced motion swaps immediately to the same selected explanation with 0 ms
  route-specific transition and animation duration.
- Without JavaScript, render all five outcome headings and explanations as one
  ordered outcome list. Enhanced controls disappear and the exact closing
  line remains.

## 9. Transformation 5 - Sequence the transition

### Section job

Explain how an ambitious operating-model change begins as bounded moves that
create evidence and preserve a way back. Keep the page out of programme-plan
theatre.

The next question is: **How does the justified intervention become a governed
build?**

### Exact accepted copy

**Anchor**  
`roadmap`

**H2**  
Sequence the change while the company keeps running.

**Body**  
The target operating model may be large. The first intervention should not be.
We sequence bounded moves that create evidence, preserve a way back and make
the next decision better informed.

**Transition sequence**

1. Define the target operating model.
2. Select one material path.
3. Establish the current state and evidence boundary.
4. Choose the smallest justified intervention.
5. Build, evaluate and release under an accountable owner.
6. Return operating evidence to the next decision.

**Text link**  
See how the intervention becomes a build

**Body**  
For an existing company, this is a migration from accumulated systems and
habits. For a new company, product, work, roles, data and software can be
designed together from the beginning. The underlying discipline is the same.

### Component assignment

`TransitionLedger`

- The H2, first body and existing-company/new-company body remain one contiguous
  `transformation.roadmap.narrative` subtree in that exact text order. The
  six-step progressive detail and action are sibling units; they may not split
  the exact narrative unit.
- The six steps form one vertical ordered ledger, never six cards, a Gantt
  chart, funnel or horizontal desktop strip.
- One numbered rule may connect the steps. It remains typographic and does not
  imitate a circuit or railway.
- The link remains adjacent to the narrative and sequence and routes to
  `/build#factory-modes`.
- The existing-company/new-company clarification remains the closing paragraph
  inside the contiguous narrative unit. It may use a 5/7 editorial split at
  desktop, but it does not become two comparison cards.
- No bitmap or icon is introduced.

### Interaction and progressive assignment

The H2, first body, link and final body remain default-visible. The six-step
roadmap is the contract's progressive detail.

If progressively enhanced, use the accepted visible label `Transition
sequence` as the native disclosure summary. No invented marketing label is

  action.

## 17. Motion, reduced motion and no-JavaScript truth

### Normal motion

- Shared navigation inherits its accepted 70-100 ms open intent, 180-240 ms
  close grace and input parity.
- Standard hover/focus/press response: 120-180 ms.
- Hero editorial settle: 1000-1500 ms into the same resolved Decision Field;
  no semantic state change.
- Decision-continuum state change: 220-300 ms. One finite eligible-desktop
  preview is allowed; it uses stable geometry, then returns to Preserve.
- No route-specific Machine Signal pass, roadmap animation or scroll-triggered
  reveal.
- Only one narrative state may change in a viewport. Manual reader selection
  always overrides the preview.

### Reduced motion

- Every Transformation transition and animation duration outside shared chrome
  computes to 0 ms.
- The current outcome readout changes immediately and preserves the same exact
  explanation, selected label and closing line.
- The hero remains a still. Roadmap and folio disclosures use native immediate
  state.
- No meaning, order, boundary or action differs from normal motion.

### No JavaScript

- One H1 and all seven sections remain in exact order.
- All exact default copy is visible.
- All five outcome explanations render in order and no inert radio/tab controls
  remain.
- All five map fields and all six roadmap steps remain readable through ordinary
  document or native disclosure semantics.
- Every contextual destination remains a real link.
- The Decision Field has intrinsic dimensions and a meaningful static state.
- No blank readout, play control, hidden panel, `aria-hidden` argument or warning
  screen remains.
