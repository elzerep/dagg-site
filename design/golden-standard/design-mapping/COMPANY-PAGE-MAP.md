## Composition

1. Position - copy-led proposition, the quiet 5:2 Decision Field seam, then
   three horizontal accountability rows.
2. Point of view - short Lift bridge from ambition to operating model.
3. Capabilities - four editorial capability statements, never cards.
4. Partnership standard - one Ink field with named decisions and boundaries.
5. Accountability - named corporate/technical owners and a bounded close.

The three exact opening rows are:

- `Decision — Name what changes, what deliberately does not, who owns the outcome and what evidence would change the recommendation.`
- `Build — Name the required context, tools, permissions, evaluations, approval and way back before release.`
- `Escalation — Return material exceptions to an accountable owner.`

The opening chain deliberately recalls the first three partnership commitments.
The progressive partnership standard retains all five commitments, including
Decision, Build and Escalation; `Five commitments · diligence boundary` remains
exact.

The complete opening chain carries `[data-company-accountability]` exactly
once. This hook is measurement-only and does not change semantics, order or
layout.

Its first two value-bearing rows carry
`[data-company-accountability-row="decision"]` and
`[data-company-accountability-row="build"]`; the final row carries
`[data-company-accountability-row="escalation"]`. The registered seam carries
`[data-company-seam]`. These hooks are measurement-only.

Company 5 keeps its exact narrative as one contiguous
`company.close.narrative` subtree. The leadership group follows it as a sibling,
remains low on Company and stays separate from both the narrative unit and the
opening chain:

- `Accountability has names.`
- `Corporate direction and technical standards have named owners. No customer name, workflow, quote or result is published without permission, provenance and a stated boundary.`
- `Direction — Christian Pérez, Chief Executive Officer`
- `Technical standards — Simon Lundmark, Chief Technology Officer`
