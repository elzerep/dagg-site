## 1. Binding interpretation

Home is one calm, warm, editorial world. It does not blend three image
directions.

- **Primary image language: Decision Field.** It establishes the company world,
  strategic judgment and the movement from imperfect operating reality toward
  an executable state.
- **Secondary image language: restrained Machine Signal.** It may make a finite
  resolution or state change legible, then it stops. It is not a second page
  world, background treatment or independent decorative asset family.
- **Operational Evidence is not a Home image family.** Home may contain a real
  coded product component or inspectable artifact. That artifact can prove a
  record, permission or state change, but it does not authorize Operational
  Evidence photography, cinematic objects, dark product-world imagery or a
  third generated visual grammar.
- **One generated image has one narrative job.** The selected hero pair is not
  reused elsewhere for decoration. A deliberate visual recall may use the same
  material logic, but not a convenient crop of the hero.
- **The page stays warm.** Paper carries the reading experience. Ink belongs to
  exact labels, contained mechanisms and the final close; it does not become a
  sequence of black technology sections.
- **Coral is judgment.** It appears where an accountable person changes the
  path. It is not a decorative highlight.
- **Sage is governance.** It appears only after a permission, boundary or stable
  resolved state exists.
- **Motion is causality or finite arrival.** The hero may settle once into its
  authored composition; the proof may show one finite causal sequence. No
  ambient particles, parallax, continuous Ken Burns movement, fake typing or
  perpetual scan is permitted.

The current frozen Home hero selection is the pair in
`slot-manifests/home.json`, not an earlier direction candidate:

- Desktop: `home-hero-decision-machine-desktop-v2`
- Mobile: `home-hero-decision-machine-mobile-v2`

Recommended rhythm at 1280 x 720:

1. Beat 1 is the only large image-led carrier.
2. Beat 2 is the shortest act and creates a clean turn into mechanism.
3. Beat 3 is a concise product index, not two mini product pages.
4. Beat 4 is the single dominant proof carrier.
5. Beat 5 closes with one decision and one primary action.

The complete Home remains within the accepted 4.5-6.0 screen-height guardrail.
No empty viewport is created merely to make the page feel premium.

## 3. Beat 1 - Position

### Exact public copy

Copy reference: `FULL-SITE-CONTENT-CONTRACT.md` -> `Home 1 - Position`.

**Eyebrow**  
AI-native transformation

**H1**  
Build the company AI makes possible.

**Lead**  
Dagg redesigns how existing companies work for an AI-native world and helps new
companies begin that way. We map the operating reality, decide what should
remain human, what can be automated and what must be rebuilt, then turn that
direction into agents or software.

**Primary action**  
Start an assessment

**Secondary action**  
See what must change

### Section job

Establish in one view that Dagg is neither a detached strategy consultancy nor
a product vendor presenting technology before the operating decision. The
reader should understand the transition, Dagg's execution role and the first
bounded action without decoding an architecture.

The next question created is: **What must change?** The secondary action routes
to Transformation.

### Visual job

Show imperfect company context meeting one material act of human judgment and
resolving into a smaller, governed operational state. The image must carry the
same causal order as the copy:

`imperfect context -> accountable judgment -> governed executable state`

The selected Home V2 pair contains the required elements:

- tactile, imperfect source material on the left;
- one enlarged coral act of judgment in the center;
- precise resolved forms on the right;
- a translucent sage boundary over the resolved state;
- enough quiet paper for the hero to breathe at delivered scale.

This is conceptual meaning, not a product screenshot. Do not add labels, arrows,
nodes, diagrams or a second visual object to explain it.

### Composition and carrier

- Desktop: the image is one full-width field beneath the opening copy, not a
  five-column illustration. Copy occupies the authored quiet upper-left paper;
  the complete source-to-judgment-to-governed-state event remains visible
  across the lower field.
- Do not place the hero inside a card or frame it like a slide.
- Do not repeat a second hero image below the fold.

### Asset and generation decision

**Use the frozen pair. No new hero still is required.**

Desktop source:
`design/golden-standard/image-system/home/home-hero-decision-machine-desktop-v2.webp`

Mobile source:
`design/golden-standard/image-system/home/home-hero-decision-machine-mobile-v2.webp`

The selected assets are resolved stills. Their one finite material-settle
entrance is editorial arrival, not semantic image motion. Do not claim that the
image itself changes operating state; the coded proof later on Home owns that
causal sequence.

### Crop and aspect guidance

- Desktop carrier: **16:10**. Preserve all three protected events - imperfect
  material, coral decision and sage-held resolution. Avoid a crop that turns the
  image into only a coral object and two rectangles.
- Mobile carrier: **4:5**, using the independently authored mobile asset. Never
  derive mobile from the desktop file.
- Mobile protected event: all source fragments, the coral decision and the
  complete governed state must appear inside the first meaningful carrier view.
- Cropping may remove excess quiet paper, but may not cut the left-hand source
  material or the right-hand sage boundary.

### Motion meaning

The full image may arrive once by moving no more than 10 px and settling from a
1.035 to approximately 1.005 scale in 1.1-1.6 seconds, then it holds
permanently. The copy may enter once by no more than 16 px. Reduced motion shows
the complete resolved composition immediately. No loop, float, pulse,
scroll-driven movement or moving camera.

## 5. Beat 3 - Technology

### Exact public copy

Copy reference: `FULL-SITE-CONTENT-CONTRACT.md` -> `Home 3 - Technology`.

**Eyebrow**  
WorkGraph + Dagg Factory

**H2**  
Strategy and execution share the same company context.

**Intro**  
Strategy often loses meaning when handed to engineering. Dagg keeps the
decision, evidence, owner and boundaries connected to what gets built.

**Shared artifact disclosure**  
Illustrative system record; not client work or a production deployment.

**WorkGraph**  
WorkGraph retains how work happens and should change: workflows, evidence,
decisions, owners and boundaries. That context remains usable through
transformation, Dagg Factory and operations.

**Text link**  
See how company context is retained

**Dagg Factory**  
Once a change is approved, Dagg Factory carries its context through design,
architecture, build and evaluation. It produces the justified agent, automation
or software with permissions, human judgment and a way back defined before
release.

**Text link**  
See how context becomes a build

### Section job

Name WorkGraph and Dagg Factory early as peer-level mechanisms, after the
business consequence is clear. WorkGraph proves continuity of company context;
Dagg Factory proves that the approved decision becomes a governed build. The
reader receives enough mechanism to choose a deeper route, not a compressed
product tour.

The next question created is: **How does the context persist?** The WorkGraph
link owns that depth; the Factory link routes to Build.

### Visual job

Use one warm editorial stage with one connected, coded product artifact that
contains two related views:

1. **Representative WorkGraph record.** Show one retained pre-decision record
   with a small number of legible fields: record identity, workflow, current
   state, available evidence and the open decision question. It must stop before
   decision, owner and boundary are resolved and must look inspectable, not like
   a graph diagram.
2. **Illustrative Factory scoping brief.** Show the same record entering a
   pre-build question with intervention options, an evaluation basis and
   permission still unassigned. It must not reveal the later result. The shared
   record identity makes the relationship visible.

These are real semantic components built from the accepted component system.
They are not generated images and do not introduce Operational Evidence as a
third page-level image family. Avoid dark cinematic framing, terminal chrome,
dashboard grids and node-edge diagrams.

### Composition and carrier

- Desktop: one shared product-index stage, with WorkGraph and Factory as two
  peer columns or two selectable states. They must not become equal-weight
  marketing cards surrounded by repeated borders.
- Each capability keeps its copy and onward link adjacent to its view inside
  the connected artifact.
- The product stage may use panel and contained ink for exact fields, but warm
  paper remains the surrounding ground.
- Mobile: WorkGraph precedes Factory in a single reading order. Recompose the
  artifact content; do not scale down a two-column desktop instrument.

### Asset and generation decision

**No bitmap generation. Build the two artifacts as accessible components using
synthetic data.**

If either artifact cannot be made legible with real text and state, simplify the
component. Do not replace it with a generated pseudo-interface.

### Crop and aspect guidance

- Component carrier, not image crop.
- Desktop stage may occupy an approximate **8:5 or 16:9** field, but internal
  content determines height.
- Mobile uses a natural vertical component. No horizontal pan is required for
  the core proposition.
- Exact labels and the shared disclosure remain at least 12 px at every target
  viewport.

### Motion meaning

Motion is optional and user-triggered. One change may show retained context
becoming available to the build:

1. a WorkGraph record is selected;
2. the approved decision and retained record become the Factory plan's inputs;
3. evaluation, permission and way-back criteria resolve;
4. the component holds.

This is restrained Machine Signal: a finite resolution, not a scanning
background. The control must have focus/tap parity. Reduced motion shows the
resolved pair. No-JavaScript shows both artifacts in the correct reading order.

## 6. Beat 4 - Execution proof

### Exact public copy

**Eyebrow**  
One operating change, carried through

From a strategic choice to a governed result.

**Intro**

**Proof title**  
Complex customer request

**Context record**

**State 1 - Operating reality**  
The request crosses systems and specialist judgment.

**State 2 - Decision retained**  
The evidence, boundary and accountable owner stay together.

**State 3 - Governed execution**  
Agents prepare a permitted action; material uncertainty stays with the service

**State 4 - Evidence returns**  
The owner decides; evidence returns and the previous path remains available.

**Text link**  
Inspect the control model

**Conditional text link**  
Inspect the evidence standard

### Section job

Prove one complete causal chain instead of adding more capability claims. The
artifact must show that Dagg carries a strategic decision through retained
context, governed action, accountable human judgment and a reversible result.

The next question created is: **How is this illustrative result controlled and
bounded?** The primary link routes to Trust. The conditional Impact link appears
only when the Impact publication gate is open.

### Visual job

Build one inspectable, coded artifact with four states and one stable identity:

`WG-041 -> operating reality -> decision retained -> governed execution -> evidence returns`

The component must make these facts visible without extra explanatory panels:

- company-approved context is used;
- the basis for the permitted next action is readable;
- the permission boundary allows an action or withholds it;
- the service owner resolves the material exception;
- the decision and evidence return to WorkGraph;
- the previous operating path remains available as the way back.

This is a product proof component, not Operational Evidence imagery. Do not wrap
it in a cinematic black object, present it as a dashboard, or add generated
proof photography. The shared synthetic-data disclosure above the connected
product artifact qualifies this example.

### Composition and carrier

- One dominant proof stage only. No row of terminals and no six-panel control
  room.
- Desktop: stable 2 x 2 four-state sequence with one state in focus and every
  state body continuously readable.
- Mobile: vertical sequence or a focusable, labelled state control. Essential
  context, boundary and way-back information must not require horizontal pan.
- The state identity `WG-041` persists across all four states.
- The same single coral judgment marker is carried through the sequence rather
  than introducing unrelated coral accents in every state.
- Sage appears when the permission boundary becomes operative and remains in
  the resolved state.

### Asset and generation decision

**No bitmap generation. Build the proof as a real component with realistic
synthetic data.**

No client logo, customer name, measured outcome or production status may be
invented.

### Crop and aspect guidance

- Component carrier, not image crop.
- Desktop target: approximately **16:9 or 8:5**, with the four-state mechanism
  legible at the delivered width.
- Mobile target: independently composed vertical state sequence. Do not shrink a
  desktop strip to 390 px.
- In reduced-motion and no-JavaScript states, all four state descriptions and
  the final governed result remain readable.

### Motion meaning

The only meaningful motion is the state transition:

1. State 1 establishes the operating reality.
2. State 2 retains decision, evidence, boundary and owner.
3. State 3 establishes the governed execution path and stop condition.
4. State 4 returns evidence so the next decision begins from a stronger record.

On desktop without reduced motion, the sequence may run once after the carrier
has been at least 60% visible for 800 ms. It never loops. Pointer presence,
focus and document visibility pause it; direct state selection cancels automatic
ownership. Run, pause and replay controls remain explicit. Mobile, tablet and
reduced-motion paths are manual-only. No-JavaScript shows all four states in
ordinary order.

## 9. Page-level motion score

The Home motion story has no more than two narrative events:

1. **Finite hero arrival.** The resolved Decision Field settles into place once
   and then holds; it does not claim a semantic before/after state.
2. **Required product resolution.** The execution proof changes
   from retained decision to governed state and then stops.

Only one event moves in a viewport. UI hover and focus responses do not count
as a second narrative event.

Reduced-motion users receive the resolved hero and complete resolved product
state immediately. No-JavaScript users receive the same five-beat argument and
all four proof states in order.

## 10. Responsive art direction

### Desktop 1440 x 900 and 1280 x 720

- Keep Beat 1 copy and the causal hero event legible together.
- Name WorkGraph and Dagg Factory before the end of the second viewport.
- Do not let either product artifact become a dense sidebar or card grid.
- Beat 4 is the single largest proof carrier after the hero.
- Maintain one warm page world and no more than one dark closing ground.

### Tablet 1024 x 768 and 768 x 1024

- Collapse the hero before copy or image becomes narrow enough to lose
  authority.
- Preserve the hero's three protected events.
- At 640-939 px, keep the frozen portrait source but remove only its excess
  quiet paper in a measured 16:9 carrier held at the protected event. The imperfect inputs, coral
  judgment and complete sage-held resolved state must remain together; this is
  measured tablet art direction, not a desktop crop.
- Recompose WorkGraph and Factory as a deliberate two-step sequence.
- Keep the execution proof's context, permission and way-back information in
  the primary reading order.

### Mobile 390 x 844 and 320 x 800

- Use the frozen portrait hero, never the desktop asset.
- Copy precedes or shares the first meaningful carrier view without covering
  the coral judgment or governed state.
- WorkGraph precedes Factory inside one connected product artifact; the shared
  synthetic-data disclosure remains visible.
- Recompose the proof vertically. Do not require pinch, zoom or document-level
  horizontal scroll.
- Keep all artifact and metadata labels at 12 px or larger.
- The final assessment action is reachable without a decorative spacer or an
  oversized empty close.
