Show one calm causal transition: imperfect company reality becomes legible,
crosses an accountable human decision and continues as a coherent
machine-speed operating state. The H1 and lead own the complete claim; the
image makes the direction instantly felt without asking the reader to decode
an art object.

transformation and engineering firm. Recreate only the image band in the
binding reference, without any typography, logo or interface. In a very wide
cinematic composition, layered torn warm-white paper and tactile fibrous

terminal, no dashboard, no icons, no Japanese-art cues, no gallery sculpture,
