# Direction B · Machine Signal

Status: complete image-language portfolio  
Medium: ImageGen raster assets  
Role: a deliberately digital alternative to Dagg's warm-paper image language

## Core idea

Machine Signal shows a company becoming more legible as work moves through evidence, judgment, build, boundary and record. The image never draws “AI.” It shows a machine-speed signal changing state.

Every image contains the same causal grammar:

`coarse signal → evidence resolves → coral decision → higher-resolution action → sage boundary → recorded echo`

The world is dark, exact and computational, but not cyberpunk. Its premium character comes from restraint, material depth and one readable consequence per image.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Image role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep ink | `#111412` | Primary field and negative space. |
| Graphite | `#242824` | Near-black relief, voxel bodies and scan depth. |
| Mineral sage | `#788071` | Governed structure, stable context and permitted boundaries. |
| Muted coral / apricot | `#E87B5A` | The one human judgment, exception or causal intervention. |
| Spectral white | `#EEF3EE` | Resolved evidence, permitted action and readable state. |

Color is semantic. Coral is never ambient lighting or decoration. Sage appears only where context stabilizes or a boundary governs action. Spectral white is earned resolution, not a glow effect.

Target balance inside an image:

- deep ink and graphite: 72–86%;
- mineral sage: 8–18%;
- spectral white: 4–10%;
- coral/apricot: 0.5–3%.

## Still-image rules

1. **One event per frame.** A still must answer one question: what changed here?
2. **Show state, not architecture.** Use signal, strata, raster passes, held exceptions and resolution changes. Never depict a technology diagram.
3. **Keep the field asymmetric.** One dominant event and one calm region are stronger than a centered emblem.
4. **Change camera logic by page job.** Hero may use deep oblique perspective. WorkGraph uses layered oblique terrain. Decision and governance use orthographic fields. Factory uses restrained isometric assembly. Impact uses repeated resolution echoes.
5. **Preserve dark air.** Do not fill every pixel with particles. At least 20% of each composition remains visually calm.
6. **Use coral once.** A second coral focus requires a second decision and therefore belongs in another frame.
7. **Let imperfection live at the edge.** Coarse voxels, lost fragments and dither remain visible before resolution. The final state is clearer, not sterile.
8. **No embedded copy.** Titles, labels, metrics and UI belong in HTML, never inside the bitmap.

## Resolution and effects

The defining effect is a controlled shift from low to high legibility.

- Low-resolution state: sparse 4–12 px voxel clusters, broken scan bands, incomplete dither and soft provenance traces.
- Transition state: locally compressed raster bands, short phosphor trails and shallow 3D relief.
- Resolved state: tighter 1–3 px signal grain, clean spectral edges and a stable material silhouette.
- Scan direction follows causality. It must never drift merely to make the background move.
- Moiré is allowed only as a short phase-change cue in the decision image.
- Bloom remains local to spectral-white resolution or the coral intervention; never wash the frame in glow.
- Grain should feel like imaging material, not a film preset.
- Do not sharpen an entire image equally. Resolution is the narrative.

Master stills are `1536 × 1024` PNG. The storyboard is `2172 × 724` PNG. Preserve these masters; create delivery WebP/AVIF derivatives later without replacing them.

## Motion grammar

Primary loop: 8 seconds, six semantic beats.

| Time | State | Motion meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.1 s | Coarse signal enters | Machine-speed work exists, but its context is incomplete. |
| 1.1–2.3 s | Evidence aligns | Source layers resolve and the material exception becomes visible. |
| 2.3–3.4 s | Coral decision | Human judgment changes the path; unchosen continuations lose resolution. |
| 3.4–4.9 s | Build resolves | The selected path assembles into a usable operational form. |
| 4.9–6.2 s | Sage boundary | Permitted action crosses; the exception is held for an accountable decision. |
| 6.2–8.0 s | Record compounds | A resolution echo returns to the field and improves the next cycle. |

Motion rules:

- Use 180–450 ms local interface/state transitions inside the 8-second causal loop.
- The camera remains stable; meaning comes from state change, not zooming through particles.
- Keep velocity in short scan passes and resolution snaps, never endless ambient drift.
- Coral appears at the decision beat and then becomes a recorded imprint; it does not pulse indefinitely.
- Sage enters only when a boundary or stable governed state is established.
- The loop must end in a state that can hold for at least 1.2 seconds and be understood as a still.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` uses the final governed frame with the prior coarse state faintly retained; no autoplay or parallax.

## Placement and cropping

- **Hero:** keep the left 34–40% dark and calm for copy. Anchor the resolution event around the right-center third.
- **WorkGraph:** crop wide enough to retain multiple context strata and the single coral exception. Do not crop into a generic texture.
- **Decision:** preserve both sides of the coral phase boundary so cause and consequence remain visible.
- **Factory:** retain at least two coarse predecessor states and the complete resolved object.
- **Governance:** both the incoming and permitted/held sides of the boundary must remain visible.
- **Impact:** preserve at least three inherited echoes; one isolated wave does not communicate compounding.
- Mobile crops must be composed from the source around the causal event, not center-cropped mechanically.

## Accessibility

- Use empty alt text when the adjacent headline and product artifact already carry the same meaning.
- When the image is the only carrier of meaning, describe the state change, not the visual style. Example: `A coarse signal resolves after a human decision and stops at a governed boundary.`
- Never rely on coral versus sage alone. Resolution, position, containment and motion state must also communicate the difference.
- Reduced motion must preserve the decision, boundary and final record in one readable frame.

## Future-generation contract

Use `01-hero-machine-signal.png` as the visual-language reference for new assets. Preserve palette, raster material, voxel scale, black level and coral restraint; change the composition and causal event for each page job.

New prompts must state:

- the page role;
- the single event being shown;
- camera logic;
- coarse and resolved states;
- what coral changes;
- what sage governs;
- required negative space;
- the full forbidden list below.

## Forbidden

- generic blue or cyan neon;
- cyberpunk, sci-fi interface chrome or holograms;
- circuit boards, chips, cables or glowing brains;
- node clouds, network diagrams or constellations;
- terminal walls, dashboards or fake product screens;
- arrows, flowcharts, growth charts or technical schematics;
- shields, locks and generic cybersecurity metaphors;
- people, faces, hands or anonymous silhouettes;
- robots, factories, rockets, planets or city skylines;
- glossy gradient ribbons and stock “AI energy” waves;
- centered logos, emblems or decorative orbs;
- text, numbers, watermarks or invented data;
- movement without a before/after state;
- repeated tunnel perspective across the portfolio;
- coral used as ambience rather than judgment;
- client-like detail that could be mistaken for evidence.
