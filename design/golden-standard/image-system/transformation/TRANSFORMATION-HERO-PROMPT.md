## Narrative job

Before technology is chosen, one accountable strategic judgment reallocates
imperfect operating reality into distinct human, agent and software/control
responsibilities inside a stable ownership boundary.

## Final production direction

Create a calm premium tactile editorial still in the established Dagg Decision
Field language. Heterogeneous operating material meets exactly one coral
rounded-rectangular judgment hinge at the edge of a stable, straight-edged sage
ownership field. Inside that field, the decision becomes three visibly
different carriers: a soft, irregular and revisable pale-paper folio for human
judgment; three repeatable charcoal-and-sage units for agent execution; and a
durable nested deep-ink plate for software/control. The coral hinge must touch
the boundary and cause the reallocation rather than float as decoration.

Keep warm paper dominant, with quiet sage, restrained ink and exactly one coral
object. Use tactile fibers, subtle imperfection and soft natural shadows. No
text, people, interface, card grid, nodes, chart, terminal, circuit, tunnel,
rail, tube, arrow, letter-like carrier, generic AI glow or unrelated
design-studio props. Reject every sperm, egg, embryo, cell, organ, seed, eye,
leaf, droplet-with-tail, phallic or other biologically ambiguous form.

Desktop is an independently authored 16:10 horizontal composition. Mobile is
an independently authored 4:5 vertical composition, not a crop, with every
protected event large enough to remain legible in a 350 px carrier.
