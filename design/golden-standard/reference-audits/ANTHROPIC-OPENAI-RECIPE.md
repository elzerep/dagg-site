# Anthropic and OpenAI reference audit — the Dagg translation recipe

Audit date: 2026-08-30  
Scope: current official Anthropic institutional homepage, Anthropic Research, Claude product overview, OpenAI homepage, OpenAI Research, OpenAI Business, navigation, conversion and representative mobile states.  
Status: reference evidence, not permission to copy either company's visual identity.

## Executive verdict

Anthropic and OpenAI solve different halves of Dagg's problem:

- **Anthropic governs materiality and tone:** warm editorial space, disciplined typography, tactile conceptual images, analytical restraint and a human register without stock-photo theater.
- **OpenAI governs product proof and conversion:** the product is available or visible immediately, real artifacts receive large visual territory, buyer language shapes information architecture and multiple intent-specific next steps are clear.

The Dagg synthesis is not an Anthropic-looking consultancy site or an OpenAI-looking product magazine. It is a conclusion-led strategic argument in a warm material world, with a real Decision Field/WorkGraph instrument that makes the product inspectable.

## 1. Anthropic institutional homepage

### Current recipe

The primary information architecture is Research, Policy, Commitments, Learn and News. The only dominant commercial action is Try Claude. This separates institutional legitimacy from the Claude product story instead of forcing one page to explain every product capability.

The desktop hero begins below an approximately 68 px navigation and occupies about 390 px. A roughly 1,285 px content field sits inside a 1,425 px document, producing about 70 px outer gutters. The approximately 61/67 px H1 occupies a dominant left column; a short serif explanation sits in the right column. There is no hero image. Typographic asymmetry is the image.

The homepage sequence is compact:

1. institutional field and principle;
2. one long dark statement about difficult questions;
3. three current releases;
4. mission plus editorial register;
5. large dark footer.

The site uses sans for conclusion, structure and navigation, and serif for judgment and explanation. The switch is semantic, not decorative.

Observed base colors include warm off-white around `rgb(250,249,245)`, ink around `rgb(20,20,19)`, lifted paper around `rgb(240,238,230)` and sparse clay/coral around `rgb(217,119,87)`. Darkness is one argument-driven pause and a footer, not an alternating tech theme.

Motion is restrained: navigation and link transitions generally occupy about 0.1–0.4 seconds; the H1 has a processed entrance, but the idea remains complete without motion.

### Dagg translation

- Use one dominant conclusion and one short explanatory counter-column.
- Let paper be a reading environment, not a beige effect.
- Use serif only where human judgment or reflective explanation is being expressed.
- Keep one contained dark argument/operations act rather than repeated black sections.
- Let motion aid reading; never require motion to reveal the basic claim.

### Do not copy

- the degree of institutional abstraction that leaves delivery invisible;
- the exact warm palette distribution, typefaces or transition treatment;
- the absence of an inspectable B2B mechanism.

## 2. Anthropic Research

### Current recipe

Research uses approximately 77 px side gutters and a roughly 1,271 px content field. The H1 is about 52/52 px. Its opening behaves like a 6/6 split: subject title to the left, remit and filtering to the right. Five research areas occupy five narrow columns.

The dominant feature image is about 837 × 471 px and consumes roughly two thirds of the field, with a news rail beside it. The image combines a real precision object, handwritten notation, grid paper and pigment. It communicates discovery, hypothesis, material intelligence and the trace of a human hand. A secondary sage image reduces its thought to one gestural mark.

The visual receives air, rules, metadata and captioning. This is why it reads as analytical rather than decorative.

### Dagg translation

- One large conceptual image per narrative chapter.
- Combine material trace with exact instrument.
- Make the object represent a business or product idea rather than illustrate the headline literally.
- Use metadata, captions and rules to make the idea accountable.

### Do not copy

- a compass, research collage or sage diagram as Dagg iconography;
- five narrow columns for homepage persuasion;
- a publication library as the homepage structure.

## 3. Claude product overview

### Current recipe

At a 1,280 px viewport, the hero is approximately 839 px tall with 59 px outer gutters. A roughly 60/66 px serif H1 occupies about 459 px while an abstract line illustration occupies the other side. A real prompt input makes the product usable inside the hero.

The narrative moves from a relationship-led promise to three capabilities, a migration action, use-case tabs with a large product artifact, further exploration, model selection and releases. Product artifacts are large and actual; model detail arrives only after the use is understandable.

Warm paper, lifted paper and white dominate. Clay marks action. A soft secondary color may appear inside the product artifact without becoming the entire brand.

### Dagg translation

- Let the Decision Field/WorkGraph instrument be operable in the hero or immediately after it.
- Organize tabs by buyer-recognizable work, not abstract technical capabilities.
- Give the product artifact hundreds of pixels and readable labels.
- Introduce technical depth only after use and consequence are clear.

### Do not copy

- a generic prompt input;
- a roughly 9,216 px product tour;
- repetitive model/card inventory.

## 4. OpenAI homepage

### Current recipe

The global information architecture is Research, Products, Business, Developers, Company and Foundation, with search, login and Try ChatGPT as utilities/actions. Labels map to audiences and tasks rather than internal departments.

The hero is product behavior rather than a marketing headline: a prompt surface roughly 700 × 72 px at a 1,280 px viewport. Prompt suggestions change over time and the first editorial block begins around y=536.

The main grid uses approximately 32 px outer gutters and a 1,201 px container. The dominant feature is about 895 px wide, the support rail about 282 px, with a 24 px gap: effectively a 3/1 proof rhythm. Feature media occupies about 895 × 503 px and support media about 282 × 282 px.

Four video surfaces were observed. Video is muted/looping where used and lives inside a story or product slot, not behind copy as ambient spectacle. Many UI transitions are roughly 0.1–0.25 seconds and explicit reduced-motion classes exist.

### Dagg translation

- Prove the product before explaining the entire company.
- Use one dominant artifact plus one narrow evidence rail.
- Put motion inside product/evidence surfaces.
- Give reduced-motion a complete, immediate resolved state.

### Do not copy

- a prompt as hero unless it performs a true WorkGraph task;
- a mixed product-and-news magazine without a controlled transformation narrative;
- media volume as a substitute for a causal argument.

## 5. OpenAI Research

### Current recipe

The approximately 46/53 px section title is followed immediately by taxonomic filters, sort and list/grid controls. Each record uses a metadata field to the left and title plus one-sentence summary to the right. The page uses no imagery and loads further records on demand.

### Dagg translation

- Impact and operational records should use explicit type/status/time metadata.
- Give each item one conclusion and one sentence of scope.
- Make evidence searchable when the library becomes large enough.

### Do not copy

- an emotionally flat white register as the homepage;
- an evidence archive without a dominant point of view.

## 6. OpenAI Business

### Current recipe

OpenAI switches to buyer-oriented Business navigation: Why OpenAI, Products, Solutions, Resources, Customers, Pricing and Contact sales. This reduces corporate/research noise for a commercial buyer.

The hero H1 is approximately 64/63 px and occupies almost the full 1,217 px container. Two conversion paths, Get started and Contact sales, appear together. Customer media and trust follow immediately.

The narrative then shows one product, buyer-recognizable teams/use cases, a unified product family, a large product artifact, segment cards and a repeated dual CTA. Use cases include Finance, Data analytics, Sales, Marketing, Operations, Engineering, Design and Security.

Measured body copy is about 17/28 px. A 282 px use-case rail sits beside an approximately 897 px media field. The platform screenshot occupies about 997 × 660 px. Segment cards are around 282 × 282 px. Color is local navigation support rather than a page-wide brand wash.

### Dagg translation

- Use buyer language, not Dagg's internal org chart.
- Pair each use case with a real, large work artifact.
- Offer two next actions: a bounded assessment and direct dialogue.
- Use named evidence before generic logos.
- Explain the product family with artifacts, not an architecture diagram.

### Do not copy

- broad frontier-intelligence language;
- all-white/all-sans sterility;
- logo trust without permissioned case evidence;
- templated video modules with weak content.

## 7. Mobile findings

Anthropic and OpenAI were sampled at a 390 px viewport. Both produced a 375 px document surface without horizontal document overflow.

Anthropic uses about 25 px side gutters and an approximately 325 px content width. Its H1 scales to about 40/44 px. The desktop hero becomes a deliberate sequence, and the mobile menu becomes a full paper canvas with large disclosure rows. Claude can move the abstract visual before copy, using image language as the immediate brand signal.

OpenAI uses about 24 px gutters. The prompt remains a usable 72 px-high surface and suggestions reflow. OpenAI Business scales the hero to about 47/48 px and retains both conversion routes in one column. Search/menu controls are about 40 × 40 px, which is below Dagg's 44 px target.

### Dagg mobile rule

Mobile is an authored composition, not a compressed desktop. The WorkGraph artifact may precede or follow the hero copy based on comprehension, but it must never shrink into an unreadable diagram. Dagg must exceed both references with 44 px targets, no overflow, visible focus and proven reduced-motion behavior.

## 8. Binding synthesis for Dagg

### Take from Anthropic

- warm paper and semantic ink/clay roles;
- conclusion-led asymmetry;
- sans for system, serif for judgment;
- one tactile idea-object at a time;
- one dark argument-driven pause;
- editorial metadata and air;
- mobile as a new composition.

### Take from OpenAI

- working product proof in or directly after the hero;
- dominant 3/1 proof rhythm;
- buyer-oriented information architecture;
- large real artifacts;
- use-case selectors in customer language;
- two intent-specific conversion paths;
- motion inside evidence and explicit reduced motion.

### Reject from both

- exact collage, symbol, palette or icon imitation;
- a fake prompt box;
- generic AI words such as `frontier intelligence`;
- sterile white catalogue pages;
- image carousels or generic players used merely to create activity;
- diagrams requiring an engineer to decode;
- any claim, client or live state without provenance.

## Evidence limits

Screenshots and computed layout establish visible hierarchy, spacing, content order, overflow and observed interaction states. They do not prove complete keyboard behavior, screen-reader quality, accessibility conformance, performance or fully effective reduced-motion handling. Dagg must verify those independently.
