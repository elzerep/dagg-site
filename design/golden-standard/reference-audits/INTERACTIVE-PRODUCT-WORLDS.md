# Interactive product worlds

Status: accepted roadmap input for P4 and the supporting-page system  
Audit date: 30 August 2026  
Authority: `../DAGG-GOLDEN-STANDARD-MASTERPLAN.md`

## The opportunity

Dagg's site should be discoverable, not only readable. A visitor should be able
to inspect how strategic context becomes a decision, how WorkGraph preserves
that context, how Factory turns the decision into a bounded build and how the
result operates.

This is not a license to fill every section with controls. Interaction is
justified only when it answers a business or product question more clearly
than static copy.

## Current Anthropic pattern

The current Claude product ecosystem gives major products their own visual and
interaction world rather than forcing every route into one repeated marketing
template.

### Claude Science

The official Claude Science page currently contains a selectable “How
researchers use Claude Science” surface with distinct states for single-cell
RNA sequencing, evolutionary analysis, protein structure and cheminformatics.
The page also presents scientific artifacts, persistent kernels, reproducible
history and domain tools as things the visitor can inspect rather than a long
capability paragraph.

Source: <https://claude.com/product/claude-science>

### Claude Design

The official Claude Design page gives the product its own world and explains
prototypes, mockups, design exploration, decks, documents and tool handoff
through product-like surfaces. The current product can create interactive
prototypes and interactive microsites, and the page uses those capabilities as
evidence for the product promise.

Sources:

- <https://claude.com/product/design>
- <https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604416-get-started-with-claude-design>

The audited page structure and source text are current. A persistent visual
screenshot of these exact interactive states remains to be added to the
reference evidence before Dagg makes a same-viewport visual comparison.

## Dagg translation

### Transformation world

Question: what changes when a company becomes AI-native?

Potential interaction:

- choose Preserve, Simplify, Automate, Rebuild or Retire;
- see which workflow, role, software and evidence changes;
- reveal why the smallest justified intervention wins;
- keep the strategic conclusion visible while product depth changes.

This is a decision instrument, not a quiz or maturity-score lead form.

### WorkGraph world

Question: how does company-specific context become usable, owned and durable?

Potential sources:

- meeting and transcript;
- Slack or another message system;
- GitHub or product-development record;
- policy and document store;
- ERP, CRM, finance or operating system;
- human decision and exception evidence.

Selecting or hovering a source may reveal:

- what is captured;
- its provenance;
- permission and retention boundary;
- which workflow or decision it informs;
- which Factory build may query it;
- what operating evidence returns to the record.

The visual must not become a node cloud or circuit diagram. The visitor follows
one named record and consequence.

### Harness anatomy

Question: what makes a model useful and governable inside a specific company?

Inspectable parts:

1. model choice and routing;
2. company context and WorkGraph;
3. tools and system connections;
4. permissions and security boundaries;
5. evaluations and alignment;
6. orchestration and monitoring;
7. operating record, escalation and way back.

The internal CPU/RAM/input-output analogy may help the team reason about the
concept, but the public interaction should lead with business function and
failure mode. Selecting `Permissions`, for example, should show what the agent
may prepare, who may release and what stops when the boundary triggers. It
should not first teach computer architecture.

### Factory world

Question: how does a strategic decision become production software or agents?

Potential inspectable path:

`decision → context received → product requirement → build plan → architecture
decision → implementation → evaluation → governed review → release`

The visitor may inspect one bounded record at each state. The page must not
expose an invented internal tool or fake live client system. It may use a
clearly labeled constructed example with synthetic data.

### Operating evidence world

Question: what happens when the system meets a material exception?

Potential interaction:

`intent → context → proposed action → boundary → accountable decision →
record + way back`

This is Dagg's strongest proof of execution capability because strategy,
technology, human role and governance become visible in one chain.

## Version roadmap

### V1 · One causal proof per mechanism

- one working decision continuum;
- one WorkGraph record with selectable source/context states;
- one Factory path with Automate and Rebuild modes;
- one operating record with pause/replay and manual inspection;
- one adaptive Build navigation flyout with a real coded preview;
- typed data and component boundaries that can support later depth.

V1 does not build a mini-app for every paragraph. It establishes the product
interaction grammar and proves that Dagg can show its method.

### V2 · Route-specific product worlds

- deeper WorkGraph ingestion and provenance exploration;
- an interactive harness anatomy;
- richer Factory pipeline and evaluation states;
- route-local navigation and examples tailored to Transformation, WorkGraph
  and Build;
- persistent shareable state where useful.

### V3 · Demonstration and sandbox

- guided assessment or customer-specific demonstration;
- permissioned sample data or user-provided synthetic scenario;
- saved or shareable operating-model recommendation;
- real product integration only where security and product maturity permit.

## Interaction laws

1. Every control answers one declared question.
2. Every state changes context, decision, boundary, action or evidence.
3. Default state carries the executive conclusion; interaction reveals depth.
4. Keyboard, touch and no-JavaScript users retain the full argument.
5. Reduced motion shows a resolved state rather than a blank or frozen middle.
6. Interaction uses realistic synthetic data and explicit provenance.
7. One primary interactive focal point per viewport.
8. No fake typing, particles, decorative 3D or productless animation.
9. A supporting page may have its own visual world, but semantic colors,
   typography roles, global chrome and Dagg's evidence policy remain coherent.
10. A visitor should be able to describe the mechanism after interacting once.

## How the three P4 image languages support discovery

### The Decision Field

Sources and decisions alter a tactile material field. Product copy and coded
records sit adjacent to the generated abstraction. Best for strategic judgment
and human consequence.

### Machine Signal

Coarse information gains resolution and cadence as context and boundaries are
added. Best for velocity, compounding capability and the transition from
human-speed reconstruction to machine-speed execution.

### Operational Evidence

Focus moves from evidence to permission boundary to accountable decision. A
real coded record is the primary object; cinematic material supports it. Best
for implementation and operating credibility.

## Acceptance questions

Before an interactive product world ships, answer yes to all:

- Does the control expose information that matters to an executive or operator?
- Is the changed state caused by the user's choice?
- Is the record and provenance concrete?
- Can the same meaning be inspected without motion?
- Does mobile recompose the interaction rather than shrink it?
- Can a keyboard user reach and understand every state?
- Does no JavaScript retain the full story and destinations?
- Is the interaction still legible without the decorative image layer?
- Does the result feel like Dagg's mechanism rather than an embedded demo from
  another company?
