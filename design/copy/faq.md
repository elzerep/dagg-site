# FAQ

## Ownership and rights

**Who owns the software you build?**
You do — the software written for you, and the model of your company that sits
underneath it. Dagg's own tooling and any third-party or open-source components
remain what they are, and are identified as such before a build starts.

**Is our data or our work used to improve anything you sell?**
No. Client material is not used to train or tune models, and it does not become
part of anything offered to another company.

## Data and access

**Where does our data sit?**
In your environment. Where a piece of work requires data to move, what moves,
where it goes and for how long is agreed in writing before it does.

**What access do you need?**
What a specific piece of work requires, asked for specifically, with the reason
stated. Access is scoped to the work and ends when the work does. There is no
standing access.

**Which model providers and third parties are involved?**
Named before a build starts. Changing them is a change you approve.

**Can we see your security and data-processing documentation?**
Yes, under NDA. The data-processing agreement, the current subprocessor list,
the data map, retention and deletion, incident handling and change control are
a single pack, kept current, and reviewed with your security function rather
than published here. A public page cannot make commitments to a reader who has
no contract, and a security answer that is not contractual is not an answer.

## The work itself

**Do you replace our existing systems?**
Only where the system is what is broken. A large part of the work leaves
systems in place and takes over the carrying between them. Replacing software
is disruptive, and it earns that disruption when the software models a company
that no longer exists.

**Who runs it after you leave?**
You can, or we can. Operating it yourself requires someone who can read the
monitoring, respond to escalations and judge when behaviour has drifted; we set
that up and hand it over, including what to watch and what should worry you.
Where that is not a capability you intend to build, the operation can stay with
us under a separate agreement.

**What if it does not work?**
A baseline is set before anything moves, so the question has an answer rather
than an opinion. Changes ship with a documented way back, and anything that
cannot be reversed is identified as such before it is built.

**How do later changes get made?**
Against the same map, through the same route, with the same records. That is
what keeps a change in year two from starting as a new discovery exercise.

## Working together

**Can we see examples of your work?**
No. Clients do not appear on this site, and that will include you. Method
questions can be answered specifically in a conversation under NDA.

**How does an engagement start?**
With an assessment: one path through the company, mapped, ending in a
recommendation — including the recommendation not to build.
