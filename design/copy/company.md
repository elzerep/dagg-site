## How we work

**Clients do not appear here.** No names, no logos, no case studies, no
measurements from real engagements. This is not a gap waiting for permission —
it is the standard. Anything we could show you about another company, we could
one day show another company about you.

**What we build is yours.** The software, and the model of your company
underneath it. Neither becomes part of something sold to anyone else, and that
sits in the agreement rather than in a conversation.

**Access is specific, and it ends.** We work from evidence a company already
generates. What we need, we ask for, with the reason stated. There is no
standing access.

**An assessment can conclude that nothing should be built.** That outcome is
delivered with the same detail as any other, and it is the reason the first
piece of work is worth buying.

## Where this work fits
Companies where decisions have to be traceable, and where the knowledge that
matters sits with people rather than in systems. So far that has meant legal,
and banking and finance. The shape of the work decides, not the sector.
