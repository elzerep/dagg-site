# Home

## H1
We map how your company actually works — then build the system that runs it.

## Lead
Most companies buy AI tools before they understand their own work. Dagg starts
with the work: how it moves, where it waits, and which parts of it are worth
changing. Then builds what follows — and runs it, where you would rather not.

## [DIAGRAM — one record, three states]

**Mapped.** How work moves through the company, including the parts nobody
wrote down.

**Read.** The agents that write the software query the map directly, so the
structure of the work is available to them rather than restated each time.

**Running.** The same map orchestrates what is live, where operating agents is
not a capability a company intends to build.

One record throughout. It is what keeps a change in year two from starting as a
new discovery exercise.

## Where this work fits
Decisions that have to be traceable. Knowledge that sits with people rather
than in systems. Processes that are regulated, or old, or both.

So far: legal, and banking and finance.

## What the first piece of work is
An assessment maps one path through the company and ends in a recommendation.
It names what should change and what should not, what a change would consist
of, who would own the result, and what would make the recommendation wrong.

It can conclude that nothing should be built. That is the outcome that makes
the rest of it worth reading.
