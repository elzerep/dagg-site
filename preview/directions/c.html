Every recommendation names what would make it wrong. A recommendation you cannot argue with is a report.

Amounts belong in the room where your company is discussed, not on a public page. What belongs here is the shape: what must change, what stays, who owns the result, and the observation that would make us withdraw it.

Why it is not a tooling problem

The hard part was never the software. It was getting the work out of the people who do it.

Christian Pérez · CEO Simon Lundmark · CTO

What a company knows about itself sits in a form nobody can query: in the judgement of the person who has done the job for nine years, in the exception nobody documented, in the meeting where a decision gets made again because the reason was never written down.

Building a workflow into software is the part that has become straightforward. Extracting it is not, and it is where an engagement actually spends its time.

That is the work we do first, and it is why the second engagement is not the first one again.
