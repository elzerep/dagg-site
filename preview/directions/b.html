The stakes

Human-organised work does not keep up with machine-speed work.

A company runs at the pace of the people carrying the work between its systems. That pace has not changed in twenty years, and it is now the constraint on everything else.

The companies that close that gap do not do it by buying tools. They do it by understanding their own work well enough to rebuild the parts worth rebuilding — and leaving the rest alone.

Human speed

Work moves when a person is available to move it. Nights, holidays and attention are the schedule.

Machine speed

Work moves when the conditions are met. The schedule is the work itself.

That gap does not close on its own, and it compounds.

Not because the technology is hard. Because getting the work out of the people who perform it is hard, and it is where an engagement actually spends its time.
