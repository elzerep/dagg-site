Start where consequence and ownership are clear.

A strong starting point has a clear owner, a material consequence and evidence the company can inspect. It does not need a predetermined technology.

Good fit

  • A workflow where waiting, rework or exceptions create a material consequence.
  • A product or operating decision where AI changes what is now possible.
  • An owner prepared to act on a recommendation or refusal.

Not a fit

  • A tool search with the outcome already decided.
  • A company-wide survey without one accountable path.
  • A request to validate a build that is already approved.

Follow the path from evidence to choice.

  1. Agree the path

    Choose one workflow or operating decision, end to end.

  2. Read the evidence

    Identify the approved sources needed to understand what actually happens.

  3. Map reality

    Capture visible work, hidden handoffs, exceptions, systems and owners.

  4. Decide

    Preserve, simplify, automate, rebuild or retire.

  5. Assemble the Decision Pack

    Write the recommendation or refusal, its boundary, owner and what would change the decision.

Before the assessment begins, Dagg identifies the evidence needed to answer the question. Do not send confidential information in the first inquiry.
