Context begins with how work actually moves.

An organization rarely has one place that explains how work gets done. The operating truth sits across meetings, documents, messages, systems and the judgment of people who know the exceptions. WorkGraph connects approved sources to the work item, handoff, owner, decision and boundary they explain.

It is not a process picture or static archive. It is a usable record of how work happens, what has been decided, what remains unresolved and what a person or agent is permitted to do next.

Why the record persists

The record grounds transformation, gives Dagg Factory approved requirements and evaluation cases, and receives evidence from operation so the next decision starts from what the company learned.

  1. Work, sources, systems, owners and exceptions become usable context.
  2. The intervention, reason, boundary and accountable owner are retained.
  3. Factory reads approved context instead of reconstructing the workflow.
  4. Review and operating evidence can update the record without replacing its identity.

Start with the context the intervention requires.

Every serious transformation needs durable company context, but the first implementation should be proportionate to the decision. A bounded intervention can begin with a focused record; the context deepens as more work is connected.
