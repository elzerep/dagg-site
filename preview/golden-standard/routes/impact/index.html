Internal staging route · not published

Two forms of transformation. One evidence standard.

Companies built

AI-native ventures designed from first principles, where product, work, software and decision rights can be shaped together from the beginning.

Companies transformed

Existing businesses where workflows, software and the operating model change while the company continues to run.

A result without provenance is a claim.

Every full proof must name:

  1. the starting condition;

  2. the strategic thesis;

  3. what changed in the operating model;

  4. how company context or a lighter harness supported the change;

  5. what Dagg Factory built;

  6. who operated the result;

  7. the verified outcome and timeframe; and

  8. the source, attribution and truth boundary.

Approved proof · none published

Publication requires an approved proof. A case appears here only when its company, Dagg's role, the evidence, the timeframe and permission to publish have passed review.
