AI-native transformation

Build the company AI makes possible.

Dagg decides how a company should work when agents and software carry more of the execution — then builds the systems that make that decision real.

Start an assessment See what must change

AI-native changes who executes — and who decides.

The decisive shift is from people carrying every handoff to people setting direction, defining requirements and resolving material exceptions while agents and software execute continuously. The gap between human-speed handoffs and machine-speed systems compounds.

Automation is not the answer to every process. The first decision is what to preserve, simplify, automate, rebuild or retire — and where a person must stay responsible.

See what must change

Start with how the company really works.

Dagg maps work across teams, systems, decisions and exceptions, including the informal handoffs no process map captures. That gives leadership the operating reality and a target operating model grounded in what AI can do now and what should stay human.

WorkGraph turns that understanding into a retained company record. It keeps the workflow, the decision, its owner and its boundary connected, so the transformation is not rediscovered at every stage.
