How we work
Policy

Clients do not appear here.

No names, no logos, no case studies, no measurements from real engagements. This is not a gap waiting for permission — it is the standard. Anything we could show you about another company, we could one day show another company about you.

What we build is yours
The software, and the model of your company underneath it. Neither becomes part of something sold to anyone else, and that sits in the agreement.
Access is specific, and it ends
We work from evidence a company already generates. What we need, we ask for, with the reason stated. There is no standing access.
We say no in writing
An assessment can conclude that nothing should be built. That outcome is delivered with the same detail as any other.
The documentation exists
Data processing, subprocessors, retention, incident handling and change control are a single pack, reviewed with your security function under NDA. More in the FAQ →
