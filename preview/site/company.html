Who is accountable

Nobody hands the work to somebody else.

In most arrangements the people who decide what should change and the people who build it are different people, in different rooms, on different contracts. That is where the cost hides: a recommendation is written by someone who will not have to make it work, and a system is built by someone who was not in the room when the reason was discussed.

Here they are the same people. A recommendation carries what a change would consist of because the person writing it would be building it. A build opens with what was deliberately not made because the person building it made that call.

It is also why the company stays small. This does not scale by adding a delivery layer, and we would rather turn work down than add one.
