We do not post roles.

The constraint has never been headcount. It is finding people who hold both halves of the work — who can sit with an operations team on Tuesday and be useful in the codebase on Wednesday, and who can tell the difference between a workflow that is painful and one that is expensive.

That combination does not sort well into a job description, which is part of why there is no listing to answer. The people we want to meet are usually not looking, and a posting is a poor way to reach them.

Write to hello@dagg.ai.

What the work is

Small engagements, few clients, and direct contact with the people whose work is changing.

Everything happens under confidentiality, which means the work is not something you can show afterwards. That suits some people and not others, and it is worth knowing before you write.

If this is recognisably your work, write and tell us what you have built — the thing itself, not the role you held while it happened.
