# Dagg Home reset audit

Date: 1 September 2026  
Viewport used for live measurement: 1280 × 720  
Publication status: local evidence only

## Verdict

The P4R6 direction gate is not fit for a Christian-facing choice. A1 has the
right strategic thesis but the wrong page architecture and a severe visible
blank-state defect. B1 has the clearest mechanism but leads with an internal
abstraction. C1 overweights governance. All three use the same nine-act spine,
similar product panels and nearly the same reading rhythm, so the purported
worldview differentiation collapses in the rendered pages.

The correct convergence is A's strategic opening with an early, concise
WorkGraph and Dagg Factory product index and one bounded execution proof.

These counts are comparative evidence, not a recipe. The decisive difference
is the peers' primary reading path: one proposition, one controlled change of
mode, a compact product or release index, and optional onward exploration.

## Peer recipes observed in the captured pages

### Anthropic

- One mission-level proposition in the hero.
- One dark, single-thesis bridge.
- A compact release index.
- One values/mission index and footer.
- The page feels short because the hierarchy is unmistakable even though the
  release and footer lists add words.

### xAI

- One product-level proposition.
- Product modes shown visually before explanation.
- One API/product proof.
- Metrics, news and a final start choice.
- Technology is demonstrated through a few large moments, not many equal
  panels.

### OpenAI

- Immediate product interaction/search surface.
- One dominant product release.
- News, stories, research and business are indexes rather than explanatory
  chapters.
- The page is longer, but the reader is never asked to hold one continuous
  nine-part argument.

### Palantir

- One cinematic enterprise thesis.
- Large product/impact moments and an explicit software portfolio.
- Strong visual authority and sparse copy inside each moment.
- Its length is supported by proprietary proof, partners and product depth;
  copying the length without equivalent evidence weakens Dagg.

## Dagg failures

1. Nine separate acts force Home to teach the whole company.
2. Repeated system records and dark panels turn execution proof into a UI
   catalogue.
3. A/B/C share the same narrative topology, so they feel like reskins.
4. WorkGraph, Factory, surfaces, trust, Impact and assessment each demand a
   fresh mental model before the reader has chosen to go deeper.
5. A1 reveal-gates essential sections and produces a huge blank full-page state.
6. Mechanically passing responsive checks did not protect against a visibly
   weak page. The prior QA verdict is therefore superseded.
