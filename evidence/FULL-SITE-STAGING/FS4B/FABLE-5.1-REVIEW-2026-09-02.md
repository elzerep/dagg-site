# Fable 5.1 full-site review — decision record

Date: 2 September 2026  
Model: `claude-fable-5-1`  
Mode: read-only, failure-first  
Initial verdict: **REJECT as release candidate; repairable without reopening the brand, Home composition or narrative architecture.**

## Scope actually inspected

Fable read the full masterplan, FS0/FS4/FS4A/FS4B packages, the complete
content contract and Home founder reset, evidence ledger, copy-density audits,
image production protocol, image catalog, asset registry, all page maps, all
peer audits, all current route sources and assembled outputs, shared chrome,
component source, assembler and snapshot server.

Fable inspected every rendered page of the three authority documents:

- Design System Foundations pages 1–15;
- Image Language System pages 1–14;
- Component and Interaction Library pages 1–17.

It also inspected the founder-selected Home reference and every then-current
desktop, tablet and mobile screenshot under `FS4B/rendered/`.

## Initial P1 findings

1. Desktop/tablet capture evidence for the longest scroll-led routes was not
   valid because full-page capture changed viewport-relative carriers, and the
   captures had no immutable snapshot ID.
2. The authored-open shared disclosure became a full-screen mobile overlay
   without JavaScript.
3. Both Home Hero action labels wrapped in the binding desktop viewport.
4. The Company Hero had a plausible sperm-and-ovum reading.
5. Transformation used a heavy sticky runway on touch widths, allowed scroll
   to override a reader's manual choice, and did not fail open when `:has()`
   was unavailable.

## What Fable explicitly accepted

- The selected Home Hero is the strongest visual on the site and preserves the
  intended causal composition.
- The global headline spine and six-act Home narrative are coherent.
- The palette, type, image-language hierarchy and restrained icon use form one
  recognizable Dagg world.
- The current semantic HTML, shared-token, deterministic-assembler and small
  vanilla-enhancement architecture is the right framework for this site.
  A migration to Next.js, Tailwind or a motion framework would add complexity
  without improving the current product outcome.
- The site reads as calm, warm, intelligent and largely exclusive; its main
  creative distance is that execution capability must be witnessed more
  clearly, not merely stated through disciplined vocabulary.

## Bounded repair response

- P1-2: repaired by limiting the viewport-covering mobile sheet to
  `html.cl-enhanced`; the no-JavaScript authored-open menu remains in normal
  document flow. The incorrect static `aria-expanded="false"` was removed.
- P1-3: repaired while preserving the founder-selected two-column composition;
  both Home actions are now single-line at 1536 and the Hero image begins at
  389 px, closely matching the reference.
- P1-4: the v1 Company pair was marked rejected. A new Decision Field v2 pair
  replaces filaments and the coral ovoid with three blunt operating fragments,
  one upright coral decision and one aligned path inside an open sage boundary.
- P1-5: the sticky scroll runway now applies only at 1180 px and above with a
  fine pointer and hover. Touch widths use direct selection; a manual choice
  remains authoritative for the page view; missing `:has()` resolves to all
  outcomes; the Transformation H1 endpoint returns to 68 px.
- Home motion was slowed from a 0.9-second sweep to a six-second finite causal
  sequence, starts only when the instrument is substantially visible, and its
  explanatory text now uses body size and stronger contrast.
- The immutable snapshot server now excludes `.claude` and
  `.playwright-cli`, eliminating the symlink refusal and unrelated test-state
  leakage from the served set.
