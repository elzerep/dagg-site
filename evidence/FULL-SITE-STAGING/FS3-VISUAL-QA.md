# FS3 Home visual and interaction acceptance

Status: **ACCEPTED**  
Date: 2 September 2026  
Immutable snapshot: `d8ffc928f43145e360c4f484c22a6d98f53011beacc5e3f9108460dea47ba533`

## Failures repaired before acceptance

1. The first mobile assembly overflowed horizontally because the closing CTA
   row retained a desktop layout. It was recomposed as one column and rechecked
   at 320, 360 and 390 px.
2. The first mobile execution trigger used an unreachable percentage of a tall
   artifact. It was replaced by a dedicated viewport-entry sentinel.
3. Card hover/focus could override an explicit Resume action. Ambient card
   pausing was removed; Pause/Resume, Replay, offscreen pause and document-
   visibility pause now coexist without conflict.

## Accepted result

- The six headlines read as one causal company story without supporting copy.
- Home contains 500 default-visible words; the opening viewport contains 41.
- The Hero uses one calm Decision Field image. Imperfect operating paths pass
  through one coral act of judgment into coherent, buildable order.
- WorkGraph is introduced only after operating reality is clear. Dagg Factory
  carries the same context into one governed execution proof.
- The dark passage is one contained four-state instrument, not a wall of
  terminals or dashboards.
- The sequence starts from scroll alone, resolves in 0.9 seconds, remains stable
  and can be paused, resumed and replayed.
- Required links and local anchors resolve. Browser console output is empty.
- Browser measurements at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px found no
  horizontal overflow, clipped essential copy or visible target below 44 px.
- Source fallbacks preserve resolved meaning for reduced motion and missing
  `IntersectionObserver`; no-JavaScript source keeps every sentence and state
  in semantic order.

## Independent red-team

The independent read-only review found no P0 or P1 defect. It rejected the
forbidden readings named by the package: the page does not resemble a
consultant deck, generic AI lightshow, design studio, Japanese-art pastiche,
Lego model, sperm/egg metaphor, terminal wall or dashboard grid.

P2 items carried into FS4:

1. Soften one level of internal boxing/mono metadata in the Act 4 instrument.
2. Tighten mobile vertical rhythm by roughly 8–12 percent without cutting the
   causal copy.
3. Consider a very small contrast/scale lift to the Hero decision point, while
   keeping it quiet and non-illustrative.
