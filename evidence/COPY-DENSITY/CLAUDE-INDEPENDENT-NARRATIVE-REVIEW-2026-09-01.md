# Claude independent narrative review - 1 September 2026

Status: read-only review completed after founder-led Home narrative repair  
Reviewer: Claude Code  
Files read: full-site content contract, rendered Home source, peer-density evidence and Home design maps  
Publication status: not approved

## Independent verdict

- Christian's two-story requirement is correct: the site needs one global
  chapter arc and a coherent local argument on every route.
- Codex is correct that every route must work as a standalone landing page,
  urgency must be a consequence rather than the voice, and advanced capability
  should be demonstrated rather than claimed.
- The requirement that headings be coherent must not become a requirement that
  headings alone contain the complete explanation. That creates long, inert
  display sentences.
- Dagg should feel early and correct rather than describe itself as bleeding
  edge. Enterprise credibility comes from precise operating judgment and
  control.

## Fail-first findings

Claude measured the pre-review repaired Home at 584 default-visible words. The
content contract allowed 420-520 while the responsive map still asserted the
obsolete 291/26/49 baseline. The map also described proof State 2 as
progressive even though the implemented and accepted causal argument required
all three states to remain visible.

Additional findings:

- urgency appeared only once and remained abstract;
- synthetic artifacts carried four separate disclaimers;
- Technology was the heaviest middle beat and slowed the page;
- the proof introduction repeated the three states before showing them;
- `Strategy and execution...` and `Strategy and engineering...` created an
  audible headline echo;
- the final Home handoff did not create the Transformation question;
- repeated assessment actions and five-outcome lists weakened the chapter arc.

## Challenge to Christian

Do not make every chapter carry all registers at equal intensity. Assign a
dominant register:

| Chapter | Dominant register |
|---|---|
| Home | Inevitability |
| Transformation | Judgment |
| WorkGraph | Permanence |
| Build | Control |
| Company | Accountability |
| Assessment | Commitment |

The heading sequence must be coherent, not sufficient without body copy.

## Challenge to Codex

- consequence-not-fear still requires a plain statement of what happens when a
  company does not change;
- synthetic artifacts demonstrate design intent, not delivery capability;
- one connected illustrative product moment is stronger than several separate
  proof simulations;
- the most credible technical evidence is the operating vocabulary itself:
  material judgment, permission attached to action, basis before action,
  accountable owner and a way back.

## Accepted response

Codex incorporated the review as follows:

- reconciled the Home contract and both Home maps;
- made all proof states default-visible;
- consolidated artifact qualification into one shared disclosure;
- shortened the proof introduction;
- changed the close to `One partner, from operating decision to working
  system.` and routed its secondary action to Transformation;
- added a flat consequence: companies still limited by human-speed handoffs
  will lose ground to those designed for machine-speed execution;
- retained WorkGraph and Dagg Factory as two views inside one connected product
  moment rather than deleting either product proposition.

The resulting rendered source measures exactly 520 default-visible words, zero
progressive words and 63 words in the Position block. This is a measured copy
baseline, not visual acceptance.
